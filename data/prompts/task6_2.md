Design a single, self-contained integrations section at 1440px desktop width for the marketing homepage of LedgerNorth.

LedgerNorth is a finance-operations platform that connects accounting, banking, payments, expenses, and workforce systems.

The section should help a prospective customer understand how LedgerNorth fits into their existing finance stack, the relevance of its integration ecosystem, what becomes possible when their systems are connected, and how to explore the complete set of integrations.

LedgerNorth supports integrations including:

- Oracle NetSuite
- QuickBooks
- Xero
- Sage Intacct
- Stripe
- Plaid
- Ramp
- Workday

Use as many or as few of these integrations as the design requires.
