Design a pre-launch landing page for Patchbay Cloud.

Patchbay Cloud is a developer platform that helps software teams test and debug webhook integrations before connecting them to production systems.

The page should help software teams understand Patchbay Cloud and decide whether to join its private-beta waitlist.

Relevant product details:

- Patchbay Cloud creates temporary endpoints that can receive and inspect webhook events.
- Developers can replay events, modify payloads, and test failure scenarios.
- Teams can share event histories and debugging sessions across engineering and support.
- The platform generates code snippets for handling captured events.
- The private beta will initially support HTTP-based webhooks and common signing methods.
