Design a single desktop web-app screen for submitting a review of an ergonomic office chair, shown to the customer 30 days after it was delivered.

The user should immediately understand exactly what they are evaluating, be able to express a quick overall judgment as well as useful detail, and understand what will happen when they finish.

Decide what information, controls, feedback structure, guidance, and level of detail are appropriate for this domain. Keep the experience focused enough to complete in one sitting.
