generate the Prospect page under the pipeline tab of a CRM used by Real Estate agents.
It's a part of the pipeline section (Pipeline > Touring).

Name of the CRM - "Bricklane"

Goals of the page:
* Provide real estate agents with a single-page overview of an active buyer client's details, status, and next steps.
* Give quick access to key actions/ workflows for the customer.

Key actions/ workflows and details in the page (not given in a particular order, you can rearrange):
* Client's basic information
  * Details to show: name, phone number, email, source
* What they are looking for
  * Details to show: budget, areas, requirements, timeline
* Home loan status:
  * Details to show: loan amount requested, % down payment, processing/ approval status, next step's timeline (e.g. expiration date if pre-approved)
* Next meeting:
  * Details to show: time, place, participants
  * Actions: Get directions to, and reschedule next meeting
* Activities eg. calls, messages, meetings, notes, listings liked (All or summary)
  * Details to show: activity type, details, date and time
  * Actions: option to show full history if full history is not present in this page, add new actions
* Open tasks
  * Details to show: task details, priority, due date
* Matched listings (All or summary)
  * Details to show: image, ask price, address, spec, % match
  * Actions: option to show full list of matched listings if not present in this page
* Others:
  * Details to show: current pipeline stage & progress (e.g., stage stepper)
  * Actions: share listing
