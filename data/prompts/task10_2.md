Design the primary desktop web-app screen for Fieldline HVAC.

Fieldline HVAC helps a commercial heating and cooling company coordinate technician schedules and customer-site visits.

The screen should help a dispatcher understand the upcoming workday, identify assignment or capacity problems, and make adjustments to the schedule.

Relevant scheduling details:

- Site visits have service windows, addresses, expected durations, equipment types, reported problems, and priority levels.
- Technicians differ in working hours, service territories, certifications, and the commercial systems they can service.
- Some visits require particular replacement parts, diagnostic equipment, or more than one technician.
- Travel time between customer sites affects which assignments are practical.
- The schedule includes planned maintenance visits, an unassigned emergency call, and a repair that is likely to run longer than planned.
- Some customer sites have restricted access times or require an onsite contact to be present.
