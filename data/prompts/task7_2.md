Design a “How it works” section for the marketing homepage of SceneLilt.

SceneLilt is a creative-production platform that helps marketing teams localize video campaigns for different languages and markets.

The section should help a prospective creative or marketing team understand how SceneLilt works and how it fits into their localization workflow.

Product details you may find relevant:

- A team uploads its master video and selects the intended markets and languages.
- SceneLilt transcribes the original dialogue and identifies spoken and on-screen content.
- The platform translates the content, generates dubbed audio, and adapts on-screen text.
- Teams can compare localized versions with the original and edit language, timing, pronunciation, and tone.
- Reviewers can comment on and approve individual market versions.
- Approved videos can be exported in the required formats for publishing.
