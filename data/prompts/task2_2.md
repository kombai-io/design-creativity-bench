generate the Candidate Deep-Dive page under the candidates tab of an ATS used by Tech Recruiters.
It's a part of the pipeline section (Candidates > Interviewing).

Name of the ATS - "TalentPulse"

Goals of the page:
* Provide tech recruiters with a single-page overview of an active job candidate's details, status, and next steps.
* Give quick access to key actions/ workflows for the applicant.

Key actions/ workflows and details in the page (not given in a particular order, you can rearrange):
* Candidate's basic information
  * Details to show: name, phone number, email, source
* What they are looking for
  * Details to show: target role, target salary, notice period/availability, work model preference
* Work authorization & clearance status:
  * Details to show: visa/work authorization type, background check status, clearance level, status timeline (e.g. visa expiration date)
* Next meeting:
  * Details to show: time, interview stage/location, interviewers/participants
  * Actions: Get calendar invite link to, and reschedule next interview
* Activities eg. calls, scorecards, emails, technical assessment results (All or summary)
  * Details to show: activity type, details, date and time
  * Actions: option to show full history if full history is not present in this page, add new actions
* Open tasks
  * Details to show: task details, priority, due date
* Matched job requisitions (All or summary)
  * Details to show: job title, department, location, compensation range, % skill match
  * Actions: option to show full list of matched jobs if not present in this page
* Others:
  * Details to show: current pipeline stage & progress (e.g., stage stepper)
  * Actions: share profile
