Design a single, self-contained integrations section at 1440px desktop width for the marketing homepage of Conduit Lake.

Conduit Lake is a data-movement platform that connects operational data sources with warehouses, transformation tools, and analytics destinations.

The section should help a prospective customer understand how Conduit Lake fits into their existing data stack, the relevance of its integration ecosystem, what becomes possible when their systems are connected, and how to explore the complete set of integrations.

Conduit Lake supports integrations including:

- PostgreSQL
- Amazon S3
- Apache Kafka
- Snowflake
- Google BigQuery
- Databricks
- dbt
- Looker

Use as many or as few of these integrations as the design requires.
