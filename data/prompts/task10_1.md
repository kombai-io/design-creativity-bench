Design the primary desktop web-app screen for Fieldline Repair.

Fieldline Repair helps a residential appliance-repair company coordinate technician schedules and customer appointments.

The screen should help a dispatcher understand the upcoming workday, identify assignment or capacity problems, and make adjustments to the schedule.

Relevant scheduling details:

- Customer appointments have service windows, addresses, expected durations, appliance types, reported problems, and priority levels.
- Technicians differ in working hours, service areas, skills, certifications, and the appliance brands they can repair.
- Some appointments require specific replacement parts or diagnostic equipment.
- Travel time between appointments affects which assignments are practical.
- The schedule includes confirmed appointments, an unassigned urgent request, and an appointment that is likely to run longer than planned.
- Customers may need to be informed when an appointment time or technician changes.
