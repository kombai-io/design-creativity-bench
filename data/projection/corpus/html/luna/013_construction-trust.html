A visible standard

Trust is a trail of evidence, not a badge.

01

Identity

Company registration, directors and trading history are matched to the person who applies.

02

Cover

Public liability, employer liability and relevant professional indemnity are checked for dates and limits.

03

Conduct

References, qualifications and unresolved complaints are reviewed by an independent assessor.
