PFAS treatment economics are turning a corner

A quieter shift in treatment chemistry is making permanent removal more attainable for mid-sized utilities—and changing the case for waiting.

Field film: Granular activated carbon beds at the Cedar Valley Water Reclamation Facility, Iowa · ClearCurrent, 14 June 2024
The conclusion

Act now on the first treatment train.

For utilities already planning carbon upgrades, locking in a modular GAC system in 2024 is lower-risk than deferring for a perfect answer. Vendor competition and longer media life have narrowed the cost gap, while public disclosure expectations are moving faster than most capital plans.
