03 / Recovery sequence

Recommended order · minimise operational disruption
  • WEEK 01Re-time the invitation window
    Offer a 06:00–22:00 booking window and test by shift, not by site.    READY
  • WEEK 02Install a fatigue check
    Require a two-question readiness check before progression is released.    REVIEW
  • WEEK 03Rebrief line managers
    Give supervisors one escalation route and a weekly exception list.    READY
  • WEEK 04Re-measure adherence
    Compare the same 14-day window; do not blend cohorts during the test.    DEPENDENCY
