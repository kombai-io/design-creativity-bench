Proof of delivery is changing

A practical briefing for dispatch leads and control-room operators on the new evidence model in Relay 4.8.

Published 14 May 2024Effective 03 June 2024 · 06:00 UTC
!

Action required by end of shift, Friday 31 May

Read the operating changes below and acknowledge that your team has completed the readiness checks.

What is changing

Four shifts to the delivery evidence record

Relay 4.8
  1. 01
    One delivery, one evidence timelinePhoto, signature, geofence and scan events now resolve to a single stop record.
  2. 02
    Photos become required at handoffCapture a parcel image for doorstep and concierge drops; the app will block completion without it.
  3. 03
    Late evidence is clearly markedEvents uploaded more than 15 minutes after the stop show an amber “delayed” label in Exceptions.
  4. 04
    Recipients can correct a nameOperators may amend a recipient name for 24 hours, with the original value retained in the audit trail.
