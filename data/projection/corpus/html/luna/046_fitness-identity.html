Your profile, your name, your access

Action required · 4 min read

Member notice · 06 June 2025

We are changing how Northstar Fitness recognises you.

01 July 2025new identity checks begin

Northstar is introducing a single member identity across clubs, classes, the mobile app and northstarfit.com. This makes your membership safer and makes it easier to move between locations. You do not need to buy a new membership or repeat your fitness assessment.

In one sentenceUse the same verified name and contact details everywhere you train.

Before 01 July, please check

3 quick actions
  • 01Confirm your legal name.
    Match the name on your government-issued ID.
  • 02Review your sign-in email.
    It must be an inbox only you can access.
  • 03Choose a recovery phone.
    We will use it only for account recovery.
  • 04Tell us about a name change.
    Bring supporting documentation to the front desk.

What you will notice

At your next visit
  • →A new green Verified member label beside your profile.
  • →One QR pass for every club on your plan.
  • →Class waitlists tied to your account, not your device.
  • →A sign-in alert when a new browser is used.

Your privacy remains the same. We collect only the identity details needed to provide membership services, prevent account sharing and meet club safety obligations. We never sell identity data or use it to judge your training.

— Maya Chen, Director of Member Experience · Northstar Fitness
