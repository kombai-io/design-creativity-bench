North America ad yield

The premium video market is holding its rate, but the shape of the quarter is changing.

One thing is asking for attention: Q2 ad yield is being carried by fewer, larger buys.

That is not a miss yet. It is a concentration risk. The current run-rate is ahead of plan, but a single sports package slipping into June would show up quickly in the weekly view.

The reading

01 / 07

North American streaming revenue is at $18.42m for the current quarter, 6.8% above the same point in April. The encouraging number is not evenly distributed: three national advertisers now account for 41% of booked connected-TV impressions, compared with 29% at the end of the prior quarter.

Field teams should read that as a timing question rather than a demand collapse. The buyers are still present, and they are paying for reach around live sport and tent-pole entertainment. The open question is whether the long tail of mid-market budgets returns before the June make-good window closes.

Where the movement is

03 / 07

Live sport is doing the lifting. The Sunday basketball package cleared at a 14% premium, while scripted prime time held close to its April floor. Mobile viewing remains the volume engine, but connected television is where the incremental dollars are arriving; buyers are accepting a narrower audience when the screen and context are guaranteed.

The practical implication for today’s route is simple: do not lead with raw reach. Lead with the quality of the next available audience and the certainty of its delivery. That is the language currently converting a cautious renewal into a larger, earlier commitment.

The risk in plain clothes

05 / 07

June is the only month that needs a human nudge. The gap is $750k, and 62% of it sits with accounts that historically decide inside ten business days. There is no evidence of a broad pullback; there is evidence that the easy renewals have already been harvested.

Watch the shape of the next two insertion orders. A clean week from the retail and travel books will lower the concentration risk. A week led only by the three national buyers will make the headline look healthier than the underlying book.
