01 / scope & provenance

What this reference is calibrated to answer

Snapshot ATL-25-0214-A

Primary question

Which transcriptional programs shift reproducibly within 24 hours of dCas9-KRAB recruitment, independent of donor background?

Included material

Four donors, two guide libraries, and 18,642 perturbation cells from the February 2025 atlas release.
