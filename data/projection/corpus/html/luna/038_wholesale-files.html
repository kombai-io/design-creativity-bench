Diagnosis

Root cause: supplier export profile changed overnight.

NorthStar's ERP export began quoting embedded commas in vendor_pack but omitted the matching quote on 8,112 records. The parser then read the next field as a unit cost, causing a schema breach. This is a source-format regression, not a pricing-rule failure.
