MC
Maya Chen18 Jun · 16:42 CDTAnalyst
The strongest finding is not that releases are being detected—it is that repeat detections cluster around equipment handoffs. Four of the seven sites in our sample had a compressor changeover, maintenance deferral or contractor transition in the preceding 30 days.
Reply from David Okafor · Operations lead
That tracks with what crews call “orphaned rounds.” A handover sheet can show a valve checked while the vapor recovery unit is still in bypass. I would add a mandatory handoff photo with the timestamp and pressure reading.
LP
Lucía Páez17 Jun · 08:13 CDTAsset owner
Can the signal distinguish a tank battery vent from a short separator upset? Our dispatch team needs a confidence threshold that does not turn every amber pixel into a truck roll.
Reply from Maya Chen · Northstar
Yes, with limits. The model combines plume persistence, wind alignment and production context. We recommend dispatch at confidence ≥ 0.78, or any second detection inside 72 hours, then reconcile against SCADA before closing.
