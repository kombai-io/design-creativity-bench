What you need to know

A concise account of the incident and our resolution.

Last updated 18 June 2024, 14:20 CET

On 14 June, the reefer unit carrying your 18 pallets of chilled raspberry purée registered 9.4°C for 47 minutes while awaiting a power connection at the Maasvlakte cross-dock. The product remained sealed and the unit returned to its configured range before unloading.

Our quality team inspected the logger, seal and pallet faces on arrival. The load is cleared for onward delivery, with an additional 24-hour hold-point and a fresh temperature check before release to your Utrecht facility.
