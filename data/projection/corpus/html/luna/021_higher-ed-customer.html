A clear view of when student aid will be released, what your finance team needs to confirm, and when funds will reach student accounts.

Your spring release dates at a glance

Applies to Northbridge University · Undergraduate and graduate programmes

CampusPay will process the spring award in four planned releases. The dates below are the dates funds leave our platform; your bank may take one to two business days to post them. No action is needed from students unless your team has marked an account for review.
