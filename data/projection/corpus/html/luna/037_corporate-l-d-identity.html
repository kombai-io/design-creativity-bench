Our learner identity is changing

A careful read before you acknowledge: Northstar Learn is moving from account records to verified workforce identities. This affects how developers, managers, and external facilitators access learning history.

Action requiredPublished 14 October 2024
!
Migration paused: 37 identities need review

The 18 October cutover cannot complete while duplicate learner records remain. Your existing courses are safe, but acknowledgement is unavailable until the review is resolved.

Verified identity, not a new profile

The learning record stays with you. The change is the key we use to recognise you across Northstar Learn and the manager reporting service.

Paused for review

The short version

What will change on 18 October?
  • Sign-in will use your Northstar workforce ID through Okta SSO.
  • Your completed courses, certificates, pathways, and due dates will be retained.
  • Manager dashboards will show one verified identity per person.
Why is the cutover paused?
  • 37 records share an email address with another learner record.
  • 11 of those records have different manager assignments.
  • Automatic merging would risk attaching compliance evidence to the wrong person.
What is explicitly not changing?
  • Course deadlines and enrolment rules remain unchanged.
  • External facilitators keep their guest access route.
  • No learning activity or assessment submission will be deleted.

How to read this

Why email alone is not enough
Shared mailboxes, aliases, and contractor addresses can look identical. Employee ID and manager relationship provide the second and third signals needed for a safe match.
Who can approve a merge?
Only the People Operations identity steward assigned to the worker's region can approve a conflicting record. Platform Support cannot override that control.

Your safe path out of the error

Do not create a second account or retry repeatedly. Use these steps so the audit trail stays intact.

Required actions

1. Check your identity details
  • Open Profile → Work identity and confirm your employee ID ends in 4821.
  • Confirm your manager is listed as Daniel Okafor.
  • Do not edit the record if either value is wrong; report it instead.
2. Report a mismatch
  • Email identity-help@northstar.example with incident NL-2471.
  • Include your employee ID and the affected course name, never assessment answers.
  • Expect a response within one business day.
3. Acknowledge once cleared
When People Operations clears the hold, return here and select “Acknowledge after review.” Your acknowledgement records the notice version and timestamp.

Recovery route

If the page keeps showing an error

A failed acknowledgement does not mean your learning record is lost. Follow this order to leave the error state cleanly.

  1. Stop retrying the acknowledgement button.
  2. Capture the incident number NL-2471 and your employee ID.
  3. Send both to identity-help@northstar.example from your work address.
  4. Return after the hold is cleared; the button will be enabled automatically.
