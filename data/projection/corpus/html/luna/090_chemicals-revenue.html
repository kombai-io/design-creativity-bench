What moves the number

Analyst reading guide

Price first, then volume

Across the set, lower realized prices are the dominant drag on reported sales. Evonik’s care solutions business also absorbed destocking in coatings and construction, while Clariant’s resilience came from a richer mix in personal care. Do not read a negative revenue bridge as a like-for-like loss of customer demand: currency and pass-through effects are material.

Mix is the useful discriminator

Arkema’s adhesives portfolio is more exposed to construction activity than the other two peers. Its advanced materials franchise, however, has a higher-value aerospace and electric-vehicle mix. That split is why the 9,200 estimate should be tested against segment commentary rather than simply carried forward from the group total.
