Will my old MHV Archive link stop working on 4 November?
Yes. At 02:00 CET the legacy archive switches to read-only. From 04 November you'll sign in to Plinth at plinth.mhv.studio using the email address held on your case sheet. We sent a single-use sign-in link to that address on 14 September.
Do I need to re-sign documents I already signed in the legacy archive?
No. Documents you've already signed — even on paper — keep their countersigned status in Plinth and don't require a fresh signature. Only the four items currently marked Pending your action need a new action.
Can my colleagues see my case file?
By default, only the named client contact and your appointed representatives see the workspace. You can add guests (architects, contractors, lawyers) at any time from the People panel; we'll email them an invitation. The studio's lead partner always retains view access.
What happens to drawings I've annotated in the old viewer?
All 1,847 annotations made between 2021 and 14 September 2024 have been exported as a separate layer (legacy-overlay.geojson) and attached to the relevant sheets. You'll see them as a toggleable overlay in the new viewer, marked with the original date and your initials.
Will the migration window affect site visits or construction activity?
No construction activity is scheduled between 02:00 CET on 04 November and 06:00 CET on 05 November. Site visits on either side of that window can proceed as planned; access to digital drawings will be unavailable during the migration itself.
Where do I send my comments on the new platform?
There's a Feedback link in the top-right of Plinth. Comments go to a small product team within the studio — Sara, Lukas, and Vesper Olsson of Holm & Ridge — and are read weekly at the platform review on Wednesdays. We respond to every comment within five working days.
