Section 02 · Schedule

Why the work is staggered like this/ 02

Cut by r.tully on 12 Nov

The schedule was cut by the build lead on the 12th against the 38-file daily ceiling you signed off in August, with three review waves stacked across the window. Wave one — texture work — runs Monday through Wednesday morning and was deliberately front-loaded because the shader team cannot start until atlases are stable. Wave two — shaders and quest scripts — opens Wednesday afternoon and lands Friday morning; this is the tightest band on the docket and is where the dependency collisions live. Wave three — localisation and audio — runs Friday afternoon through Sunday and was deliberately left loose so the localisation lead can absorb drift without forcing a re-cut.

You have not adjusted the schedule since the 14th, though you have flagged that the moss_packed re-bake will push the texture close-out roughly four hours against the original plan. The shader side still has a six-hour buffer against the wave-two start, so a single re-bake cycle fits comfortably. If the ASTC slice needs a second pass, however, the buffer collapses and wave-two starts against an unstable atlas — at which point the renderer team should be told to fall back to the 0.42.6 shaders rather than review against an in-flight texture.

Section 05 · Reviewer notes

What the reviewer has written down/ 05

Drafted by halverson-vance, 14:31 GMT

The third pass on the moss_packed atlas looks correct on the ETC2 path. The mip chain is in order, the atlas UVs match the new terrain shader, and the channel ordering is consistent with the other Ember Reach assets. The Switch-side ASTC fallback, however, has not been re-baked against the new UVs, and the runtime budget is still exceeded by 1.3 MB — the runtime team's profiling at 23:14 last night showed a 1.4 GB working set on Switch during the swamp approach, against the 1.25 GB ceiling we agreed in October. The fix is mechanical — re-bake the ASTC slice against the new atlas — but it has to land before the wave-two shader work starts, because the tide_fog shader reads from these atlases.

The lighthouse chain script lighthouse_chain_001.lua references a removed collider, ent_lighthouse_chain_clip, which was deleted in the 0.41 line. The script will silently no-op when the player approaches the chain, and the lighthouse event will not trigger. This is treated as a red-class collision against the quest script and has been escalated to d.silvers; the fix is to replace the collider reference with ent_lighthouse_chain_phys, which is the new physics collider introduced in 0.42.0.

The wetland_loop_mastered audio asset has a 7-frame phase misalignment against the player's walk cycle, flagged by the audio lead. The asset is otherwise within spec — levels are correct, the loop points are clean, the wet/dry crossfade holds — but the misalignment will produce an audible comb-filter on the third beat of every other step. Re-master requested.
