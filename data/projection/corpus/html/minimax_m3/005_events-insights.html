Insight #2197 Severity · Watch

VIP conversion drift across the autumn trade-show calendar

First detected Tuesday 9 December, 14:08 GMT · Tracking window 12 Sep – 9 Dec · Owner Anouk Verbeeck

In the trailing ninety days the share of trade-show enquiries that convert to confirmed attendance has slipped from 31.8% to 24.1% — a relative drop of roughly one quarter that the bureau considers worth surfacing before the spring commitments are taken. The movement is concentrated in delegate passes priced between £640 and £1,180, where the conversion gap is widest; mid-tier and complimentary categories have held steady within their usual noise.

The drift does not appear in registration numbers themselves — completed bookings are within 4% of the comparable 2023 cohort — but in the ratio of enquiries that reach the payment stage. Anonymous funnel captures between the venue selector and the invoice step suggest delegates are leaving to compare agenda detail elsewhere rather than abandoning outright; average session depth before exit has lengthened by 38 seconds, which is consistent with a comparison shopper rather than a bounce.

The bureau's reading is to slow the checkout into two explicit pages, expose the per-session cancellation terms earlier in the venue selector, and to test whether a single-sentence "most delegates lock in within nine days of enquiry" reassurance reduces the comparison detour. Anouk has flagged a manual sample of 47 lost enquiries for the editorial board to walk through on Thursday at 16:00 GMT.
