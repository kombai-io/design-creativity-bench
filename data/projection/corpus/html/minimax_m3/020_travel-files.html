02The problem

The cost of disorder.

Travel documents today are scattered across inboxes, personal safes, family-office SharePoints and the glove compartments of cars. The disorder is not theoretical — it has a measurable annual price tag for executive households.

  • 67% of trip disruptionoriginates from missing, expired, or mis-categorised documents. Forrester Travel Operations Survey, November 2025.
  • $48,200 average costof a single document-related incident, including rebooking, ground handling, legal review and lost board time.
  • 14 hours per monthconsumed by executive assistants chasing paperwork across inboxes — time taken from strategic work.
  • 4.2 trips per quarterrequire an urgent out-of-hours document rescue, averaging 3.6 hours of principal time per rescue.
  • 1 in 9 principalshas travelled without a required visa or credential in the past 24 months — frequently without knowing it.
  • $1.84m annual exposurefor a typical 6-principal household under current arrangements, prior to Atlas Files intervention.
Audit finding A spot review of 22 prospect households in December 2025 found an average of 17 material document risks each — most commonly expired ESTAs, single-entry Schengen visas used twice, and vaccination records failing to meet destination entry rules published in the preceding 30 days.
03The solution

The Atlas Files solution, four surfaces.

The platform operates as four interlocking surfaces, each governed by the same role-based access regime. Select a surface to inspect.

The Vault

Every passport, identity document, credential and certificate held in a single encrypted record, with biometric binding to the principal at intake.
