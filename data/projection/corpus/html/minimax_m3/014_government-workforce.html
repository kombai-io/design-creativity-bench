§ 2 Three measures, side by side

The Plan submitted by the Cabinet Office on 23 September 2024, the Plan as confirmed by the Council on 14 October 2024, and the net change after the three amendments that were put to the vote. The Council has no power to alter the Plan other than by formal amendment under Rule 9.

01 / 03

Total establishment Full-time equivalent posts

Proposed 47,210FTE
VS
Confirmed 47,820FTE
47,210 proposed 47,820 confirmed
+610 ↑ +1.29% · restoration of 410 health posts and 200 justice posts after Commission scrutiny
The Cabinet Office submitted an overall reduction of 1.3% on the FY2024 baseline. The Council returned the Plan with restoration of posts the Cabinet Office had identified as "deferrable" but which the Council judged operationally critical in-year.

Schedule A, paragraph 2: net additions must be drawn from the contingency reserve (ringfenced £47.6m); no re-profiling permitted beyond 30 June 2025.
Source: PSWC/PLAN/2025/03 · Annex A
02 / 03

Senior Civil Service Posts at SCS pay bands

Proposed 4,255SCS
VS
Confirmed 4,180SCS
4,255 proposed 4,180 confirmed
−75 ↓ −1.76% · two SCS bands merged and 75 posts regraded to Grade 7 / SEO after Amendment 2
The Cabinet Office's draft would have left SCS headcount broadly flat against FY2024. The Council, on the advice of the Senior Salaries Review Body, judged that two SCS bands (B and the lower A-band equivalent) were duplicative at the current span of control.

Schedule A, paragraph 5: regrading to be completed by 31 March 2025; no compulsory redundancy; transition protections per SSRB circular 2024/09.
Source: PSWC/PLAN/2025/03 · Annex C
03 / 03

Digital specialists % of total workforce (FTE)

Proposed 14.2%
VS
Confirmed 16.5%
14.2% proposed 16.5% confirmed
+2.3 pp ↑ +2.3 percentage points · Amendment 1 carried: digital fast-stream intake +220, mid-career re-skilling for 540 generalists
The Council heard from the Institute for Government (Dr Mei-Lin Cheung, public question 03) that the Plan would have left the UK central-government workforce 2.6 percentage points below the OECD median by FY2026. Amendment 1, moved by Councillor MacIntyre and seconded by Dr Vasquez-Ortiz, was carried 5–2.

Schedule A, paragraph 3: Cabinet Office to publish a digital pipeline delivery plan by 31 January 2025, including named accountabilities.
Source: PSWC/PLAN/2025/03 · Annex D

Meeting detail

Quick reference for administrators

Resolution 247-B — final text EXTRACT

"The Council, having considered the Workforce Plan for the financial year 2025–26 submitted by the Cabinet Office on 23 September 2024, and having heard from the Permanent Secretary and from members of the public in respect of items 2 and 3 of the agenda, confirms the Plan subject to the three amendments set out in Schedule A and the directions set out in Schedule B, and directs the Secretary to lay the confirmed Plan and the accompanying Equality Impact Assessment before Parliament by no later than 1 November 2024."

Amendments — how they ran

Amend 1 Digital specialists mix (MacIntyre) Carried 5–2. Moved the Plan from 14.2% to 16.5%.
Amend 2 SCS banding restructure (Vasquez-Ortiz) Carried 6–1. Regraded 75 posts to Grade 7/SEO.
Amend 3 Probation service posts (Kareem) Lost 3–4. Out of scope under Rule 9.2(c).
