01 — Reading guide

How this page is organised

A short reading guide for when you come back cold, written by Anwen for Anwen (and anyone else who needs it).

This page is set up for the moment you return to it after a few weeks away and need to pick up where you left off. The three figures shown alongside one another are not a ranking — they are three views of the same programme, each useful in its own right. The most recent closed quarter sits in the centre, the one before it on the left, and the same three months a year earlier on the right. Read them as three witnesses to the same story, rather than a leaderboard.

Everything on this page is drawn from the attendance log kept on the shared drive, which Anwen reconciles against the volunteer sign-in sheets on the first Monday of each month. If a number here looks wrong, the most common cause is a session that was rescheduled across the month boundary. The log posts to the date the session actually happened; the volunteer sheet sometimes records the original booking. Check there before adjusting anything here.

What you see below is the first closed quarter for this reporting format, so the figures are deliberately sparse. The structure is in place so that, when the next quarter closes, you will not need to learn anything new. Where a column is empty, the heading and the comparison still serve as a reminder of where the data will appear.

03 — Reference

Where things are kept

An index, in case you have forgotten where things live between visits.

Most of what is shown here lives elsewhere; this is the index. The full session log is in the shared drive under Horticulture / Attendance / 2026. Volunteer hour tallies sit beside it in a separate sheet, broken down by week. Referrals and the consent paperwork that goes with them are kept in the locked filing cabinet in the back office and are deliberately not represented on this page.

If you need to change a number, change the source rather than the figure on this page — the page regenerates from the source on the first of each month and any direct edit here will be overwritten. Three things it is easy to forget: the website contact form pulls through on the first, the volunteer rota refreshes on the Monday, and the trustee report is built from a snapshot taken on the last working day of the quarter. None of these are adjustable from this page.
