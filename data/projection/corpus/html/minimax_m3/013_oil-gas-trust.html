What happens when I press Activate?
  1. The trust record is locked for edit and countersigned by the trustee on behalf of Halton & Verret. You will be emailed a countersigned PDF.
  2. The beneficiary roster is committed to the disbursement engine. From this moment, adding or removing beneficiaries requires a restated agreement (median turnaround 9 business days).
  3. The operator (West Pecos Operating Co.) is notified that the trust is armed and ready to receive production revenue on the next settlement cycle.
  4. The first cutoff is the last business day of December 2024, for distribution on 15 January 2025. Schedule K-1s and the plain-English summary go out the same day.
