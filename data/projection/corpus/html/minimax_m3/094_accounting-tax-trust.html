Practice statement

Margaret counsels individuals, families, and charitable organisations on the formation and ongoing administration of revocable and irrevocable trusts, charitable gift vehicles, and closely held business succession plans. Since joining Carradine in 2014 she has administered more than $1.4 billion in trust assets and serves as named fiduciary on thirty-two client relationships across four U.S. jurisdictions.

Her practice concentrates on generation-skipping transfer planning, charitable lead annuity trusts, and special-needs fiduciary structures, with particular depth in cross-border matters for U.S. persons with treaty-resident beneficiaries. Margaret holds a J.D. from the University of Virginia School of Law and an LL.M. in Taxation from NYU School of Law; she is a Certified Trust and Fiduciary Advisor and an active member of the Maryland and District of Columbia bars.

She is a frequent presenter at the American College of Trust and Estate Counsel's regional conferences and currently serves on the grant review committee of the Bethesda Community Foundation. This revision adds her 2024 appointment to the firm's fiduciary risk committee.
