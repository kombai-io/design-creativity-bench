Zone 4 Ambulance Service Contract — Renewal Decision

$12.4M three-year renewal value, Zone 4 ambulance service

On 14 January 2024 the Emergency Medical Services Authority received a three-year renewal proposal from Westside Ambulance Service, Inc., the incumbent provider for EMS Zone 4, covering the Westgate, Riverside, and Hill District service areas and approximately 184,000 residents. The proposal was submitted under §4.2 of the expiring contract and reviewed jointly by the EMS Authority, the Office of Procurement, and the Office of the City Attorney across seventy-three working days.

The proposed contract carries a three-year value of $12.4 million, structured as a base transport rate of $1,047 with a volume adjustment clause tied to the published Consumer Price Index for Medical Care. The rate represents a 6.8% increase over the expiring contract, attributable primarily to fuel and personnel costs documented in the vendor submission and corroborated by the National EMS Advisory Council regional survey of February 2024.

Performance against the expiring contract has exceeded baseline expectations. Average Priority 1 response time across Zone 4 for the trailing twelve months stands at 7 minutes 24 seconds against a contractual ceiling of 9 minutes, an improvement of 41 seconds over the prior contract period. Clinical quality measures, scored against the National EMS Quality Alliance framework, place the zone in the second-highest performance band for cardiac arrest survival to discharge, at 14.2%.

The Office of the City Attorney has reviewed the renewal for compliance with the contracting authority delegated to the EMS Director under §3-101 of the City Charter and has flagged two items for negotiation prior to execution. The first is a clarification of the response-time measurement protocol to address recent disputes over clock-start conventions. The second is the equipment-replacement clause, which currently permits staggered replacement and should be revised to a defined replacement schedule tied to vehicle age and mileage.

Recommendation

Approve the three-year renewal of the Westside Ambulance Service contract in the amount of $12.4 million, conditional on the two clarifications identified by the Office of the City Attorney. Authorise the EMS Director to execute the renewal on behalf of the City upon satisfaction of those conditions, with execution to occur no later than 14 July 2024 to ensure continuous service coverage from the contract commencement date of 01 August 2024.

Decision status Awaiting — routed 29 May 2024

Background and timeline

EMS Zone 4 has been served under successive three-year contracts since 2011, with Westside Ambulance Service as the incumbent for two consecutive contract periods. The current contract (EMS-2018-0731) commenced 01 August 2018 and was extended through 31 July 2024 by amendment EMS-2022-44, authorised by the City Manager in November 2022 to permit the procurement review cycle to conclude.

Service-area context

Zone 4 encompasses 41.6 square miles of mixed residential, light-industrial, and riverfront terrain, including a 14-mile stretch of the Northbank Greenway trail and the Westgate Rail Corridor. Call volume in the trailing twelve months averaged 178 transports per day, peaking at 244 during the August 2023 heat event. Mutual-aid activations with adjacent Zone 5 occurred on 17 occasions, all inbound.
