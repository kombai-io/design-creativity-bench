About this panel

Relay is a tenant-scoped control plane for fleet communications hardware. Devices in the field push telemetry, dispatch state and push-to-talk channels over LTE-M and NB-IoT to a regional gateway; the gateway terminates the radio link, authenticates the device and forwards traffic to the operations console over mutual TLS on port 9443.

This embedded panel is the read-write control surface for the Carrigan Logistics (CL-014) tenant. It shows the live device fleet, channel topology and gateway routing configuration. Changes propagate on the next device heartbeat (≤ 60 s) without rolling firmware. Use it for routing decisions, dispatch tuning and post-incident review — not for customer-visible settings.
