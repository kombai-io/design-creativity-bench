01 What is Linework

about · 2 min read

Linework is a unified telecom operations console for distributed teams. It replaces your patchwork of PBX, softphone, call-center, and ticketing tools with one workspace that handles SIP trunks, hunt groups, call recording, and post-call analytics end to end.

Field engineers, dispatchers, and on-call responders route voice, video, and messaging through the same secure fabric — across 14 carrier regions and 9 PSTN jurisdictions — without giving up carrier-grade reliability.

What you get on day one
  • Carrier-grade SIP trunks · BYOC supported core
  • ACD hunt groups with sub-second failover core
  • SRTP voice · WebRTC video · presence media
  • ServiceNow · PagerDuty · Jira · Linear · Zendesk integrations
  • Per-leg MOS, jitter, and packet-loss telemetry analytics
  • SOC 2 II · GDPR · HIPAA · CALEA-ready compliance
