Settlement with Mathematical Certainty.

Veritas is a distributed ledger protocol designed for high-frequency capital markets. We replace clearing house trust with cryptographic proofs, achieving T+0 finality at scale.

A Developer's Perspective on Trust

Atomic Swap Architecture

Our protocol ensures that delivery vs. payment (DvP) occurs in a single atomic transaction. Either both legs of the trade execute, or none do. No counterparty risk, no intermediary reconciliation required.

Sparse Merkle Trees

Verification is performed using SMTs, allowing developers to verify the existence or non-existence of a specific asset state without downloading the entire 4TB ledger history.

Immutable Audit Trail

Every transaction on Veritas generates a Zero-Knowledge State Proof (ZKSP). Skeptical developers can run the v-verify tool against any transaction hash to independently confirm validity.
