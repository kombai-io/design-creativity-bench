Post-Incident Analysis — Generator G2 Trip Event Root Cause Summary

Root cause analysis commissioned following the unscheduled trip of Generator Unit 2 at Generation Station Beta on 2025-01-08 at 03:42 UTC. The unit tripped on differential protection with no fire, no abnormal noise, and no operator-initiated shutdown. Initial investigation of the protective relay recordings showed a bolted three-phase fault at the generator terminals lasting 0.18 seconds before breaker isolation. Disassembly of the generator terminal box following the cold shutdown (completed 2025-01-09) revealed melted copper bus bar material at the Phase C connection lug, indicating a high-resistance loose connection that progressed to arcing under sustained load conditions of approximately 87% of rated capacity.

Corrective actions implemented: (1) All terminal lugs torque-checked and retorqued to specification per GE Bulletin GB-HD-2023-017; (2) Thermal scan performed on all phases with ΔT reading of 2.1°C indicating uniform condition; (3) Insulation resistance megger-tested — all phases above 850 MΩ; (4) Protective relay settings verified against the coordination study dated 2023-06-15; (5) Updated preventive maintenance checklist item PM-G2-TERM to mandate infrared thermography at each routine interval. Case closed under RCA-2025-0108-G2 with no recurring deficiency noted.
