SECTION 06

Appendix: Glossary & Methodology Notes

Definitions and methodological notes supporting the calculations and classifications used throughout this report.

Glossary

Trust Integrity Score
Weighted composite of six sub-domains: Governance Audit (25%), Financial Transparency (20%), Grievance Resolution (20%), Beneficiary Reporting (10%), Inter-Agency Cooperation (10%), Whistleblower Safeguards (15%).
Platinum Tier
Entities scoring ≥95.0 on the Trust Integrity framework, audited annually, entitled to designate one voting representative on the Council of Delegates.
Gold Tier
Entities scoring 88.0–94.9, audited biennially, with advisory representation at the Council.
Silver Tier
Entities scoring 82.0–87.9, triennial audit cycle. Provisional standing — must achieve Gold-level metrics within 3 years.
Bronze Tier
Entities scoring <82.0 or newly admitted. Bi-weekly monitoring. Must complete mandatory remediation programme within 18 months.

Methodology Notes

  • Portfolio values reflect end-of-year audited balances in GBP. Currency conversions applied at ECB spot rates as of 31 December 2024.
  • "Attrition" includes voluntary withdrawals, mergers, wind-downs, and revocations of membership.
  • Trust scores are normalised to a 100-point scale using the ATN standard deviation adjustment factor (σ = 4.2).
  • Grievance resolution timeliness measured from formal filing to written disposition. Median resolved in 23 calendar days (interquartile range: 14–38 days).
  • Funding allocation figures sourced from member returns filed via the ATN Secure Portal (v3.4). Not yet externally verified.
  • All composite charts exclude data for entities currently in flag status until remediation is complete.
