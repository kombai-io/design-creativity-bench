Phase 4 Migration: NFC Token Standardisation Across Southeast Corridor Network

Effective 1 July 2025, all Level 3 and Level 4 smart card credentials issued on the legacy Mifare DESFire EV1 platform must be migrated to EMVCo-compliant NFC tokens (type 4 compliant). Operators are required to acknowledge this directive within 14 business days and begin processing the identity transfer queue by 15 July 2025. Affected batches comprise approximately 47,820 registrations spanning the Chatham Main Line, Great Eastern Regional routes, and the Thames Gateway electrified corridor. Non-compliance will trigger an automatic freeze on new card provisioning per TfRM Section 7.4(a).
