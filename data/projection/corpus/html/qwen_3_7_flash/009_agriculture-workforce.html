Document the operational arc of the six-month period. This means chronological entries describing planting windows, irrigation events, chemical applications, mechanical harvests, and post-harvest handling. For each event, record the following: the exact date range, the acreage involved, the machinery deployed, the workforce assigned, and any deviations from the planned schedule. When the mid-March cold snap forced us to pivot from tomato transplanting to emergency cover-cropping of mustard and ryegrass on four hundred acres of the north tract, capture that decision-making chain — who proposed it, who approved it, how quickly the seed contracts were fulfilled.

Pay particular attention to the interface points between operations and workforce. The most useful operational narratives are the ones where supply constraints, weather events, and personnel availability converge into a single pressure point. These are where the developer readers will find actionable intelligence about systemic fragility.

This is the section where optimism goes to die, and that's precisely why it matters. Compliance reporting must address every applicable regulatory framework without exception: OSHA labour standards (including the mired specifics of the Agricultural Employee Protection Act amendments effective April 2024), pesticide application recordkeeping under EPA Phase IV protocols, water-use permits governed by the Western Interbasin Compact provisions, and the state-level migrant housing inspections administered by the Department of Consumer Affairs.

Each compliance domain should receive an honest assessment. Mark it as compliant only if you can produce the paper trail within forty-eight hours.
