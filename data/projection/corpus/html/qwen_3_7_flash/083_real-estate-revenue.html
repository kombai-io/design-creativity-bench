Quick Tour of Meridian
Meridian aggregates revenue data across your entire real estate portfolio. Here's how to navigate:

Left rail — Switch between portfolio, revenue, tenants, billing, and settings views.

Filter bar — Narrow data by date range, property type, currency, and active tags. Click the × on any tag to remove it.

Revenue tabs — Toggle between monthly, quarterly, yearly, and property-level breakdowns. Each tab has its own controls.

Configuration panel — Set defaults for reporting, recognition methods, and board deck branding. Changes save automatically when you click Save.
