North Campus — Primary Wing Evacuation Plan

Route arrangement reviewed for clarity, capacity adequacy, and compliance with District 14 General Order 2024-08 on emergency egress from K–3 instructional blocks.

Current Status

The North Campus primary wing evacuation plan routes approximately 320 students and 28 staff members through two designated corridors along the eastern exterior of Building C. Corridor A leads to the athletic field assembly zone via the west loading bay gate; Corridor B channels occupants toward the north parking lot secondary muster point. Both corridors were mapped during the February 2024 walkthrough conducted by Facilities Director Priya Anand. Initial compliance scoring placed the arrangement at 87 of 100 against the revised Trust & Safety Standard v3.2, with the most notable deduction arising from insufficient alternate-way signage near classroom block 4B—where seasonal vegetation encroachment was observed to obscure two of four identification markers.

As of the latest site visit on March 12, 2025, all previously flagged obstruction issues have been remediated. Vegetation clearance extended 12 metres beyond the documented boundary, and photoluminescent path markers have been installed at six new positions identified during the reassessment. Pending final sign-off from the District Safety Committee, this plan is expected to move from "Under Review" to "Approved" status within the current evaluation cycle.

Findings Summary

Reviewer Margaret Reeves conducted an on-site audit on March 12, 2025, accompanied by Buildings & Grounds Manager Thomas Eklund. The team traversed both primary egress paths during operational hours and timed the movement flow using stopwatches across three separate drills. Additional notes were taken regarding the condition of exit hardware, corridor width clearances, and the placement of audible evacuation alarm speakers relative to noise-generating equipment (kitchen ventilation hoods, music room instruments).

Three corrective actions have been logged and assigned. Action CA-2025-0312-A requires the replacement of faded directional decals on the interior wall of corridor C-East before April 1. Action CA-2025-0312-B calls for repositioning the wheelchair transfer ramp at the south stairwell landing to comply with ADA slope standards; currently the incline measures at 1:10 rather than the required 1:12 minimum. Action CA-2025-0312-C mandates an updated accountability roster template posted at Assembly Zone Alpha to include non-verbal check-off methods for students who may experience communication anxiety during drills.
