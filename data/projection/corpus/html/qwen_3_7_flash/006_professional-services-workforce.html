Methodology & Notes

This report is generated automatically from the time-allocation ledger on Friday evenings and is reviewed each Monday morning before being pushed to the partner distribution list. Each collision entry is tagged with a confidence score derived from how recently the underlying commitments were entered — entries older than fourteen days carry a lower confidence weight because resources may have been re-assigned since the original booking.

When you see a double-booking, it means two distinct engagements have been assigned overlapping calendar time to the same resource within a single working day. A capacity overflow means the sum of committed hours for a resource this week exceeds their contractual maximum. A dependency block indicates the resource isn't necessarily double-booked, but their output is required as an upstream input for another engagement whose deadline cannot shift — effectively creating a soft conflict on the critical path.

All monetary risk figures represent estimated revenue exposure if a collision leads to missed delivery dates, per the contract value recorded against each engagement. These figures do not account for penalty clauses, goodwill discounts, or reputational impact. They are a floor.

How resolutions work

Click Resolve on any row to open the resolution dialog. You will be shown three suggested actions: defer the lower-priority engagement to the next available slot, transfer a portion of the work to an adjacent-capacity colleague, or escalate to the practice lead for a discretionary decision. Each option includes a brief cost analysis. Once a resolution is applied, the collision moves to the resolved register and does not appear in future runbooks unless the underlying commitment is modified.

If you spot a discrepancy between what this report shows and what you know is happening on the ground, use the flagging link at the bottom of any row to submit a correction. Corrections are reviewed within one business day by the resource planning team, and once accepted the source record is amended retroactively. You'll receive an email confirmation when your flag is processed.
