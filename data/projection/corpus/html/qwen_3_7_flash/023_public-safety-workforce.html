Active Incident Reports & After-Action Reviews

14 Jun 2025 — 03:47 EDT

Chemical Runoff Event, Cedar Creek Bridge

HazMat team 12 responded to a tanker derailment carrying industrial solvents near the Cedar Creek crossing. No casualties reported. Evacuation radius set at 800 metres. Crew deployed Class-A respiratory suits.

Photo credit: Sgt. T. Okoro, North River FD — scene documentation #HD-7741
11 Jun 2025 — 18:22 EDT

Multi-Vehicle Collision, I-95 Mile Marker 217

Four-vehicle pileup during evening rush caused partial lane closure for 3 hours. EMS Unit 7 administered triage at the scene. Two patients transported to Lakewood Regional Hospital in stable condition.

Dash-cam footage available upon request — File ref: LWR-MVC-22058
28 May 2025 — 11:05 EDT

Wildfire Outbreak, Blackwood Forest Reserve

Dry lightning strike ignited approximately 120 hectares of mixed hardwood forest northeast of Blackwood. Fire crews established containment lines along county roads 14 and 29. Air support requested for aerial water drops.

Aerial surveillance still — Drone Op 44B by State Forestry Division
19 May 2025 — 06:33 EDT

Structure Fire, Maplewood Apartments Block 7

Morning structure fire in a six-unit residential complex. All residents evacuated without injury. Cause under investigation — suspected electrical fault in unit 7B. Adjacent units assessed for smoke damage.

Scene overview — Arson investigator present, case file AR-5592
22 Apr 2025 — 22:15 EDT

Flash Flooding, Lowland District B — Summit Ridge

Rapid-onset flooding from dam release schedule error displaced 340 residents. Emergency shelters activated at the community centre and St. Brigid's Hall. Water receded within 18 hours. Damage assessment ongoing.
