⚠ Mandatory Policy Update: Revised Cancellation Terms Effective July 2025

All membership agreements will be updated to reflect the new cancellation notice period of thirty (30) calendar days, effective July 1, 2025. Members currently on the previous fourteen-day cycle will receive a prorated credit for unused days during the transition window (July 1 – July 31). No action is required from members unless they wish to exercise their cancellation right before June 30.

Please review the full terms document linked below and ensure your team contacts are briefed ahead of any incoming customer calls starting next week.
