What Is SafeHarbor?

SafeHarbor is a cloud-native community emergency response platform built for municipalities, fire departments, and emergency medical services. It digitizes the entire chain from citizen incident report through unit dispatch to post-incident analysis.

The platform was developed following a three-year pilot across five county jurisdictions, involving direct collaboration with the National Association of Statewide Telecommunications Associations and alignment with NIMS/ICS standards.

  • Real-time GIS-based unit tracking and nearest-resource dispatch algorithm
  • Citizen mobile application for SOS reporting with photo/video upload
  • Automated priority scoring using APDAC triage protocol engine
  • Interagency cross-channel messaging (CAD, AMS, RAS systems)
  • End-to-end audit trail compliant with CJIS security requirements
Fig. 1 — Command Center overview displaying multi-agency situational awareness (live demo capture, Springfield PD HQ, March 2024)

Key differentiator: Unlike legacy CAD systems that require expensive hardware installations, SafeHarbor runs entirely on existing agency smartphones and tablets with LTE connectivity. No new radio infrastructure is required.

Citizen-to-Dispatcher Journey

Step 1 · Under 30 seconds
Citizen Opens App & Initiates Report
User opens the SafeHarbor mobile app on iOS or Android, presses the SOS button, and automatically shares GPS location, environmental audio recording, and pre-loaded medical profile if available.
Step 2 · Under 15 seconds
Priority Triage Engine Assigns Acuity Score
The APDAC scoring engine evaluates reported symptoms, location severity factors, and concurrent nearby incidents to assign an urgency score of 1 (Life Threatening) through 5 (Non-Urgent). This occurs server-side without operator intervention.
Step 3 · Under 45 seconds
Nearest Available Unit Dispatched
GIS routing identifies the closest available units by real-time GPS tracking. The dispatcher reviews the automated recommendation—one tap to confirm—and unit is notified via push notification on their department-issued device.
Step 4 · Ongoing
Real-Time Status Sharing & Communication
Citizen receives live ETA updates, photos of responding unit, and can share additional context through the app. Dispatcher and field unit maintain encrypted voice/text channel. All communications logged for post-incident review.
Step 5 · Post-incident
Automated After-Action Report Generation
System compiles a complete incident record including timestamps, unit assignments, communication transcripts, location traces, and outcome data into a standardized PDF report within 60 seconds of case closure.

Implementation Roadmap

Our phased rollout methodology ensures minimal disruption to ongoing operations. Each phase includes dedicated on-site change-management support and a rollback guarantee window. Below is the current implementation schedule for the Metro County deployment.

Phase 1 · Completed Oct 2024
Infrastructure & Data Migration
Cloud tenant provisioning, legacy CAD data migration (3 years of records), HIPAA security audit, and inter-agency credential federation setup.
Phase 2 · Completed Nov 2024
Dispatcher Pilot Program
12 trained dispatchers operated SafeHarbor alongside legacy CAD in parallel mode for 30 days. Dual-system validation confirmed data integrity across all 847 test incidents.
Phase 3 · In Progress Jan 2025
Full Deployment & Go-Live
All 47 dispatch positions migrated to SafeHarbor. Legacy CAD retained as backup for 90 days. Citizen app public launch begins with targeted SMS campaign to 200K registered residents.
Phase 4 · Planned Mar–Apr 2025
Advanced Features & Optimization
APDAC triage tuning based on first-quarter operational data, predictive surge modeling for seasonal incidents, and integration of wearable IoT vitals monitoring for first-line responder safety.
