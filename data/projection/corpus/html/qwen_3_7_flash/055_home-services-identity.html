Maddison & Co. was founded after Elena Maddison spent eight years working as a field technician for Puget Sound Utilities, where she developed a reputation among her supervisors for tackling jobs that other contractors avoided—particularly the ones involving water intrusion into heritage buildings and the subsequent structural remediation that followed. In 2014 she incorporated, secured her general contractor's license, and began taking residential and light-commercial projects directly. She carries certifications through both the Air Conditioning Contractors of America and the National Association of Water Damage Restoration Technicians.

The firm has maintained a 4.94 average across 312 reviewed jobs since 2014, with no completed projects requiring rework. Maddison personally visits every job site twice during the course of major restorations—once within the first forty-eight hours and again before sign-off—and the accompanying inspection reports are included upon request. All subs are either former utility colleagues or recommended by trade associations, and Maddison carries their insurance certificates before any work begins.
