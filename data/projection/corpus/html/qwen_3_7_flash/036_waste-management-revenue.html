Built for the coordinators who keep operations running

You don't have time for spreadsheets. CircularLedger does the legwork so you can focus on the people.

Live route revenue tracking

Connect your fleet data and watch estimated recoveries update as drivers complete runs. See exactly which routes pay and which bleed money.

Market-price feeds

We pull spot prices from ICIS, Fastmarkets and local brokers — no more calling four different recyclers just to set a floor price.

Team accountability logs

Assign ownership per material type and per route. Drivers get credit for quality sorting, supervisors see throughput reports.

Invoice automation

Generate buyer invoices from logged volumes, apply contract rates, send PDFs or EDI — one click from data to payable.

Compliance-ready exports

One-click EPR and landfill diversion reports aligned with EU Waste Framework Directive. Auditors asked for details next month? You're covered.

Onboarding in hours, not weeks

Import your existing routes from CSV or connect FleetFlow or Wasteco in two clicks. Our team handles the rest during business hours.

Go live in three straightforward steps

No consultants required. Your team starts using it the same day.

1

Upload your route list

Drop in a spreadsheet — even a messy one. We'll parse stop addresses, vehicle types, and current haul agreements automatically. Takes about seven minutes for a mid-sized fleet.

2

Match off-takers to materials

Our assistant suggests buyers for each material class based on your location and volume profile. Accept, reject, or swap — you stay in control of pricing relationships.

3

Start logging & invoice

Drivers submit end-of-shift photos or quantities via the mobile app. You review, approve, and generate invoices within the platform. First payment can land in under ten business days.
