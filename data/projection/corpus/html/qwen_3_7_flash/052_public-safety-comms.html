Audit Narrative

Overall Assessment

This incident required a coordinated multi-agency response over an extended period. The initial dispatch from DCR-Ctrl was prompt and comprehensive, correctly identifying the hazard classification and sending appropriate HazMat resources.

  • HazMat-1 provided timely atmospheric readings, enabling proactive shelter-in-place directives before wind shifts compromised adjacent communities.
  • Police cordons were established efficiently, preventing civilian ingress to the exclusion zone.
  • The Major Incident declaration was made within 34 minutes of initial alarm — within expected parameters for a facility-based hazardous materials event.
  • Evacuation of Eastfield Primary School proceeded without incident; all 147 pupils and staff accounted for.
Noted Concerns

Two areas warrant attention in post-incident review:

  • The wind-shift advisory at 04:24 introduced a new affected zone but took approximately eight minutes to reach all frontline units. Consider reinforcing secondary broadcast channels for re-directed shelter zones.
  • Tanker-3's water supply relied on a single site hydrant; if this had failed, the deluge operation would have been disrupted within ninety minutes. Redundancy planning should be flagged for the next joint exercise.

Findings Summary
Communication discipline was strong throughout. Initial dispatch contained all essential intelligence (What, Where, Priority, Resource). HazMat atmospheric reports were precise and directly informed civil protection decisions. The major improvement opportunity relates to alerting reassigned zones — consider implementing an automated push-notification trigger linked to weather service API inputs rather than relying solely on verbal re-broadcast.
Recommended Actions
  • Schedule follow-up workshop with Eastfield Petrochemical Works ERT (Emergency Response Team) regarding integration of plant-level radio links with county DCR infrastructure.
  • Brief Operations Division on dual-water-supply redundancy for future site-specific plans at high-risk industrial premises.
  • Update Shelter-in-Place re-broadcast protocol for wind-driven incidents (Task Group lead: PC T. Okonkwo).
