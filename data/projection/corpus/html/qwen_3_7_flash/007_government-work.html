Preparation Checklist

Please ensure the following items are available prior to the inspector's arrival:

  • Current WHS Policy and Management System documentation
  • Register of Hazardous Substances (SDS sheets for all substances)
  • Incident and injury register for the past 12 months
  • Emergency evacuation plan and drill records
  • Asset registers for all firefighting equipment
  • Recent asbestos register (if applicable)
  • Forklift operator licences and training records
  • Machinery maintenance logs for compressors and pressurised vessels

Required site preparations:

  • Ensure all fire exits are unobstructed and clearly signed
  • Assign a staff member to accompany the inspector during tours
  • Provide safety gear: hard hat, hi-vis vest, steel-cap boots for inspector
  • Secure the diesel storage area for review of bund walls and spill kit
  • Make the electrical switchboard room accessible
  • Prepare the first-aid room for inspection of supplies and registers

Important Notice Regarding Diesel Storage

During the pre-inspection questionnaire return, new diesel fuel transfer infrastructure was declared at your premises. This triggers an additional assessment requirement under Regulation 45 of the WHS Regulations 2011.

The following conditions apply due to the newly declared hazardous installation:

  • The inspector will require photographic evidence of the bund wall capacity (minimum 110% of maximum stored volume).
  • Spill response procedures must demonstrate a maximum response time of 15 minutes from detection.
  • The underground pipework diagram must be current and show all isolation valve locations.
  • Staff training records for operators of the fuel transfer system must cover competency validation dating back no more than 24 months.
