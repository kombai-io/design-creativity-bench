Submit Application

Complete the organizational profile with EIN, primary denomination, founding year, and governing body structure. Upload your Articles of Incorporation.

2

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Prospective Members

Is Servant's Hand Network affiliated with any specific denomination?
No. We are an independent 501(c)(3) organization registered under Fellowship Missionary Enterprises. Our advisory board includes leaders from Baptist, Methodist, Presbyterian, Anglican, Pentecostal, and non-denominational traditions. We do not endorse, certify, or ordain anyone—we facilitate professional employment relationships across the broad Christian workforce ecosystem.
What types of roles do you typically see posted?
Ranging from senior pastor, worship director, youth minister, and chaplain positions to administrative roles such as communications director, facilities manager, and development officer. We also host postings for campus ministry coordinators, prison chaplains, hospital liaisons, and international missionary coordinators. Recently we've seen a 34% increase in nonprofit management and program director roles within parachurch organizations.
Do you serve organizations outside the United States?
Absolutely. While our headquarters is in Nashville, Tennessee, we actively serve organizations in 67 countries. Our international module handles currency conversion, local labor law references, and visa sponsorship documentation for foreign-hired clergy and staff.
How long does the verification process take?
Typically 5–7 business days from complete submission. Incomplete applications—missing EIN confirmation or denomination endorsement letters—can extend this timeline. We send automated status emails at each stage of the review.
Can individual job seekers register without an employer account?
You can create a candidate profile for free, but to apply for positions you will need an invitation link from a verified employer organization. We do not currently allow independent job seekers to browse the full listings—this protects employer privacy and maintains our vetting quality bar.
What happens if my ministry loses its 501(c)(3) status?
Your account enters a 60-day grace period during which you may not post new positions but can manage existing ones. After 60 days without updated tax-exempt documentation, all active postings are suspended pending re-verification or voluntary closure.
What is the typical cost-per-placement?
Based on our annual benchmarking study covering 3,847 organizations in FY2024, the median cost per successful placement (including employer subscription amortization) was $2,140. For comparison, using external recruiting firms averaged $18,500 per clergy placement. Our internal data suggests a savings ratio of approximately 8.6:1 versus traditional search consultants.
Can I track how many people viewed my postings?
Yes. The Steward and Sentinel plans include real-time impression tracking, click-through rates by demographic segment, and application completion funnel analytics. Essential plan employers receive a weekly email digest with aggregate numbers.
Do you offer placement guarantees?
We do not guarantee a hire, but we do offer a 90-day replacement window at no additional fee. If a placed candidate voluntarily leaves within 90 days, we will repost the position at no extra charge and provide up to three new pre-screened alternatives drawn from the same theological match pool.
How do you handle abuse allegations related to hires made through the platform?
We maintain zero tolerance. Any confirmed substantiated abuse allegation triggers immediate account suspension, reporting to relevant state authorities and the National Clearinghouse on Clergy Sexual Abuse, and notification to all organizations that received an applicant from the flagged account. We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.
Are salary disclosures required?
No—but 78% of employers who disclose salary ranges receive 3.2× more applications and reduce their time-to-hire by an average of 11 working days. Our housing allowance calculator (included in Steward and above) helps pastors and employers negotiate compensation packages that comply with IRS Publication 517 guidelines.
What data do you collect about candidates?
We collect standard application data, credential records, and self-reported theological and denominational preferences. We never sell candidate data to third-party marketing firms. Candidates may request deletion at any time under our privacy protocol, which honors GDPR-equivalent requests for international members.
Do you offer an API for integrating with existing HR systems?
Yes—the Sentinel plan includes REST API access to our posting, applicant tracking, and credential databases. We provide SDKs in Python and C#, comprehensive OpenAPI 3.0 specifications, and webhook support for real-time event streaming. Common integrations include BasketConnect, Planning Center, and Peachjar church management platforms.
Is there a mobile app available?
We offer a progressive web application (PWA) optimized for tablets and phones. Employers can approve/reject applications, message candidates, and publish new postings from mobile. A native iOS app is scheduled for release Q2 2025; Android follows in Q3.
How is my data backed up and secured?
All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Daily incremental backups and weekly full dumps are stored in geographically redundant AWS regions. We conduct annual penetration testing by a third-party firm and maintain SOC 2 Type II certification.
