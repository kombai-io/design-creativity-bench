RTE-001 — North Ridge Health Outreach

Overview

This route covers maternal health awareness and vaccine uptake promotion across three primary healthcare clinics along the North Ridge corridor. The outreach team conducts daily house-to-house visits supplemented by scheduled clinic-based seminars every Tuesday and Friday. Printed educational materials are distributed alongside oral briefings conducted in Twi and English.

Performance Metrics

Metric Target Actual Variance
Household visits 4,200 4,580 +9.0%
Seminar attendance 1,800 1,640 −8.9%
Materials distributed 6,000 6,220 +3.7%
Positive responses 65% 71% +6pp

Reviewer Findings

Dr. Osei reports strong household penetration but notes inconsistent seminar turnout — likely due to competing traditional events during certain weeks. She recommends shifting seminar days to Wednesdays starting 23 October to improve alignment with community calendars. No data integrity issues found. Approved for Phase 2 rollout pending the day-shift adjustment.
