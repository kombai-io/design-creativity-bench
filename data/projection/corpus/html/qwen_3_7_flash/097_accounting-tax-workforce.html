Getting Started
1
Configure base parameters. Set fiscal year, report method (avoidance vs. no-avoidance), and reclassification thresholds in the Configuration panel above.
2
Assign wage types to classification slots. Drag existing benefit categories from the Reclassification Slots panel into priority order. Highest-Gross-Up items first.
3
Define exclusions. Navigate to the Exclusions tab to filter out exempt employee groups before simulation.
4
Run simulation. The model will calculate projected FBT reduction percentage and total dollar exposure against your configured parameters.
Tip: Reordering wage types by drag-and-drop shifts priority weighting during simulation. Items listed higher receive greater reclassification probability in the optimisation algorithm.
