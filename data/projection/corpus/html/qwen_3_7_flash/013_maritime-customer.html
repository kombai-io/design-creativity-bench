5 Incident Logs

Reported Events & Investigations

Transparency in incident reporting is central to our safety culture. The events documented below have been recorded under the mandatory IMO/GISIS reporting framework and reviewed by the Aegira Safety Committee. Full investigation reports are available upon request to account holders.

  • Date: 2025-03-12  ·  Classification: Non-Injury Property Damage  ·  Severity: Low

    During unberthing operations at Terminal T4, Port de Barcelona, a minor shearing fracture was detected in starboard bow fairlead casting. No structural compromise to hull plate. No crew injuries. Vessel departed for Valencia dry dock on 2025-03-15 with temporary shoring in place.

    Cause determined as metal fatigue at weld joint origin — casting manufactured by Schottel GmbH under batch SC-2021-B. Replacement unit sourced and installed. Root cause analysis filed with RINA under case RINA-IR-2025-0891.

    Diagram 5.1 — Location of shearing fracture on MV Wavewalker starboard bow fairlead. Photographic evidence retained in Case Folder IR-2025-0312.

  • Date: 2025-04-19  ·  Classification: Machinery Anomaly  ·  Severity: Medium

    Third engineer reported sustained resonant vibration in engine room at frequencies between 45–52 Hz while transiting Mediterranean at 17.5 knots. Vessel reduced speed to 14 knots and altered course to maintain ETA. Shore-based engineering consultation initiated via satellite link at 03:42 UTC.

    Diagnosis points to cavitation in secondary fuel injection pump nozzles — Pump Bank 4, Unit 2 suspected. No damage confirmed during subsequent port-call examination at Genoa (2025-04-22). Spare parts ordered from MAN Energy Solutions, Stuttgart. Installation scheduled for dry dock appointment.

  • Date: 2025-06-01  ·&;nbsp; Classification: Propulsion Loss  ·  Severity: High

    Stern azimuth thruster failed to respond to bridge commands during maneuvering in rough seas approaching Bristol Channel. Manual override engaged for 4.5 hours. Port tugs summoned as precautionary measure when channel entry required higher thrust output than remaining propulsion could deliver safely.

    No collision or grounding occurred. Incident reported under GISIS protocol category P3. Preliminary findings suggest hydraulic coupling shear-pin failure. Investigation ongoing.
