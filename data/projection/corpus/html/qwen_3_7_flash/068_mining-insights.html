Getting started

If this is your first time using the directory, here is the path from zero to a functional register. Each step maps to a single responsible party, so there should be very little handoff friction.

  1. 1

    Contact the operator. Reach out to Elspeth Rawlings at Operations Support and request access to the directory admin interface. You will receive a provisioning link within two business days.

  2. 2

    Import your site roster. Upload the CSV export from your HRIS system. The template expects columns for full name, email, job title, site code, and discipline area. The validator will flag any rows with missing fields before submission.

  3. 3

    Verify and publish. Review the preview screen for completeness, confirm the count against your internal headcount, and click Publish. Entries become searchable immediately after publication.

  4. 4

    Schedule a review. Set a quarterly review cycle so site leads can approve updates, add new starters, and deprecate transfers out. Automation reminders can be enabled from the same panel on the right.

Scope and governance

The directory currently covers three operational regions: the Pilbara iron ore complexes, the Athabasca Basin uranium corridor, and the Grasberg copper–gold district in Papua. Additional sites will appear in the registry once their respective regional managers complete onboarding.

All records are treated as internal documents. Access is restricted to authenticated users within the corporate directory. Fields marked sensitive — such as personal mobile numbers or home addresses — are excluded from search results and visible only to authorised administrators.
