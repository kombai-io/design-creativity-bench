Questions? Quick answers.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or switch clouds anytime from your dashboard. Changes are prorated instantly — no calls, no fees. Most teams start with Deployment to automate releases, then expand to Both.

How does annual billing with 20% off work?

Prices shown are per month. With annual billing you pay 20% less per month, billed once per year. Example: Both Deployment is $3,600/mo monthly, but only $2,880/mo ($34,560/year) annual — you save $8,640/year. 30-day money-back still applies.

Is there a setup fee or charge today?

No. No setup fees, no hidden costs, and no charge today. You’ll enter payment details securely in the next step (Step 3), and you can cancel before your trial ends.

Why do most teams choose Both?

Both gives you AWS + Azure in one dashboard for 10% less than buying separately ($1,800 vs $2,000 for Monitoring; $3,600 vs $4,000 for Deployment). You get unified deploys, cross-cloud failover, and one invoice — future-proof if you expand.
