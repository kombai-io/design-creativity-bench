↗ HOW IT WORKS

From chaos to confidence in three steps.

STEP 01

Create an endpoint

Run npx patchbay init or click New Endpoint. Get hook.patchbay.cloud/pb_… instantly. Point Stripe, GitHub or any provider at it.

STEP 02

Fire events & inspect

Every delivery is captured with headers + body + signature. Verify HMAC, pretty-print JSON and see exactly what your code will receive.

STEP 03

Replay & ship

Replay to localhost:3000, mutate fields, inject failures — then export a tested handler. Share the session and merge with confidence.

No — that’s the point. Patchbay creates isolated temporary endpoints. Point a test or sandbox provider at Patchbay, debug safely, and only wire production when you’re confident. No prod credentials ever required.
Private beta supports HTTP-based webhooks with JSON payloads and common signing methods including HMAC-SHA256, timestamp-tolerance checks and shared secrets. That covers Stripe, GitHub, Shopify, Twilio, Slack and most modern providers.
