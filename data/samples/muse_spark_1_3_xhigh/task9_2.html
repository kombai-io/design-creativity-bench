The program

Practitioner talks on the hard parts of production AI

No hype keynotes. Five focused tracks go deep on the decisions, trade-offs, and post-mortems behind real deployments — so you leave with patterns you can use on Monday.

📊
Track 01

Model evaluation that matters

Design evals that predict production behavior. Learn how teams build golden sets, score open-ended outputs, and track quality over time instead of chasing one-off benchmarks.

  • Offline → online eval alignment
  • Human + LLM-as-judge done right
🔎
Track 02

Retrieval systems in the wild

What breaks when RAG meets messy enterprise data. Chunking, hybrid search, re-ranking, and measuring whether retrieval actually improved the answer.

  • Debugging retrieval failures
  • Cost vs. quality trade-offs
⚡
Track 03

Inference: latency, cost & scale

Serve models without burning budget. Practitioners share batching, caching, routing, and GPU strategies that keep p99 low and finance happy.

  • Small vs. large model routing
  • Caching & guardrails at scale
🤖
Track 04

Agents that don’t fall over

From demo to dependable. Tool design, planning limits, human-in-the-loop, and observability for multi-step agents operating with real permissions.

  • Tool & memory patterns
  • Testing non-deterministic flows
🛡️
Track 05

Production reliability

Monitor, rollback, and recover. Drift detection, prompt & model versioning, incident response, and SLAs for systems that can’t just “retry.”

  • Monitoring beyond uptime
  • Safe rollout & rollback
🎤
How it’s taught

Short talks. Real artifacts.

30-minute practitioner sessions with code, dashboards, and failure stories. Q&A is moderated and technical — bring your architecture questions.

Don’t miss · Live on stage

Live failure-analysis: why did it work in testing and break in production?

Our most talked-about session. A practitioner team replays a real AI application incident — same prompts, same code — and shows step-by-step how it behaved differently in testing versus production.

  • 1See the divergence live. Test logs vs. production traces, side-by-side, with data drift and user behavior you didn’t plan for.
  • 2Follow the diagnosis. Eval gaps, retrieval misses, and silent model changes — isolated in real time with audience hypotheses.
  • 3Leave with the fix. Concrete guardrails, eval additions, and monitoring checks you can copy.

Good to know

Deciding if Modelcraft is for you?

Short answers to the questions we hear most.

Who should attend?
Engineers building and operating production AI systems — ML, applied AI, platform/infra, and engineering leaders who own reliability, cost, or quality. If you’re shipping to real users, you’ll fit right in. It’s not an intro-to-AI event; sessions assume you’ve built or operated a model-powered feature.
Where in Chicago does it take place?
Downtown Chicago, within walking distance of Union Station and CTA lines. We share the exact venue, transit, and hotel block with registered attendees — and we’ll help with accessibility or travel questions at hello@modelcraftsummit.com.
What does registration include?
Everything: all practitioner talks, the live failure-analysis session, the hands-on evaluation lab (with sample outputs and datasets to keep), small-group office hours, lunch with dietary options, coffee all day, and the evening gathering. No separate workshop fees.
Do I need to bring anything to the evaluation lab?
Just a laptop and charger. We provide sample model outputs, datasets, starter notebooks, and scoring rubrics. You don’t need to bring proprietary data — but you’re welcome to bring redacted examples for office hours.
How do office hours work?
Small groups of 8–10 meet with a speaker or experienced AI engineering leader for 30–40 minutes. Sign-up opens at morning check-in on a first-come basis. Bring a specific problem — an eval that isn’t predictive, a retrieval miss, a latency budget — for the most useful conversation.
What’s the refund policy?
Full refund until September 30, 2027. After that, tickets are transferable to a colleague at no cost — just email us. If we must cancel, all tickets are fully refunded.
