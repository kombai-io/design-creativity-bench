01 — Live on stage

The Live Architecture Teardown

How does a real production system evolve as usage explodes and a 6-person team becomes 60? Our guests walk through every major inflection — live diagrams, real timelines, honest regrets.

● prod-us-central • livev4.2 → v7.0
$ evolution --from monolith --to platform
├─ 2019: single deploy, 6 engineers
├─ 2022: 40 services, on-call pain
├─ 2024: platform team + paved roads
└─ 2027: multi-team, self-serve & SLOs

With Q&A: challenge every decision in real time.

  1. See the before & afterArchitecture at each growth stage, with traffic and org charts.
  2. Hear the why behind the rewriteCosts, incidents, and developer friction that forced change.
  3. Steal the playbookPatterns you can apply to your own platform next week.
02 — Hands-on

Incident-Response Simulation

Join a table, pick a role — communicator, investigator, commander — and work a realistic outage together with coaching from seasoned responders.

No experience required. Facilitators guide every table — perfect for onboarding new on-call engineers.
