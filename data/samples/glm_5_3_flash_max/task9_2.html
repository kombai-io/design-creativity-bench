Why this summit exists

A day built around the gap between demo and production.

Most AI conference talks stop at the demo. Modelcraft Summit starts where that talk ends: the model is deployed, the traffic is real, and the failure modes are yours. If you own an AI feature in production — or you're about to — this day is for you.

You'll leave with

  • An eval suite you actually built, tested against real sample model outputs during the hands-on lab.
  • Field-tested patterns for retrieval quality, inference cost, agent guardrails, and reliability — from engineers who run these systems daily.
  • Names and numbers. Small-group office hours mean you'll know exactly who to email when your pipeline misbehaves at 2 a.m.

Questions

Before you register

Who is Modelcraft Summit for?

Engineers building or operating AI systems in production — ML engineers, backend and platform engineers working with models and LLMs, and tech leads who own reliability. The talks assume an engineering background; we skip the "what is AI" slide entirely.

Is there a virtual or streaming option?

No. Modelcraft Summit is deliberately in-person — the evaluation lab, small-group office hours, and hallway conversations are the point of the day. Talk recordings and slides are shared with registered attendees afterward, but the interactive sessions exist only in the room.

What do I need for the hands-on evaluation lab?

A laptop with a browser. Sample model outputs and datasets are provided, and every exercise runs in the browser — no local setup, no SDK installs, no accounts. If you'd rather observe, lab walkthroughs are projected at the front of the room.

How do the small-group office hours work?

Speakers and experienced AI engineering leaders each host a table, and you rotate through 45-minute sessions in groups of roughly eight. Sign-ups open at morning registration, so arrive early if there's someone specific you want to talk to. Bring your architecture diagrams.

What's the refund and transfer policy?

Full refunds are available through September 18, 2027. After that, you can transfer your registration to a colleague at any time until October 9, 2027 — just reply to your confirmation email with their name.

Where exactly is it, and when does it start?

Downtown Chicago, a short walk from the Loop; the exact venue address is included in your registration confirmation. Doors open at 8:30 AM CDT on October 16, 2027, sessions begin at 9:15, and the evening gathering runs until 9:00 PM.
