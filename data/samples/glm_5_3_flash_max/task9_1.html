Is this for you?

Built for the people close to production.

Stackglass Live is deliberately small and deliberately honest. Here's a straight answer on whether it's worth your Saturday.

Come if you…

  • Build, run, or pay for an internal platform — and want to see how other teams evolved theirs
  • Own SLOs, on-call, or incident response and want tactics, not theory
  • Are pushing developer-experience changes and need evidence to back them up
  • Lead a platform, infrastructure, or reliability team and want candid comparisons with peers

Maybe skip it if you…

  • Want vendor booths or product demos — there aren't any, on purpose
  • Are after 101 tutorials; sessions assume you've shipped to production
  • Need a multi-track mega-conference — this is one room, by design

The program

A day built around real production.

Four formats, one thread: how real systems are built, run, and kept alive. Nothing here is a disguised sales pitch.

Talks

Practitioner talks

Eight 15-minute talks with live Q&A across internal platforms, reliability, infrastructure, and developer experience. Real systems, real numbers, real tradeoffs — including the decisions that didn't work out.

Mainstage

Live architecture teardown

A real production system, taken apart layer by layer on stage: how the architecture, the traffic, and the engineering org grew together — what aged well, what didn't, and what they'd do differently now. Audience questions steer the last stretch.

Hands-on

Incident-response simulation

Ninety minutes, small teams, one safely broken system. Triage, communicate, and recover together against the clock — then debrief how your team's real incident process holds up under pressure.

Ask anything

Small-group office hours

Speakers and experienced platform leaders host small-table sessions to close out the day. Bring the messy stuff: migrations, adoption, on-call, platform teams nobody uses. No stage, no slides.
