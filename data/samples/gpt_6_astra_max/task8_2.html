A few notes on the private beta.

What can I test in the private beta?

We're starting with HTTP-based webhooks and common signing methods, including HMAC signatures. Capture and inspect events, modify payloads, replay deliveries, and test failure scenarios—all before connecting to production.

Does Patchbay replace my production infrastructure?

No. Patchbay is your pre-production workbench, not a production delivery service. Use temporary endpoints with test events and non-sensitive sample data, then connect your finished integration to your existing production systems.

Is this just for engineering teams?

Developers will feel right at home, but debugging rarely happens alone. Shared event histories and sessions let support work alongside engineering, with the same payloads, replay results, and context in front of everyone.

How do beta invitations work?

Join the waitlist with your work email. We're inviting teams in small batches so we can onboard thoughtfully, hear your feedback, and build around real integrations. An invitation will include everything you need to get started.
