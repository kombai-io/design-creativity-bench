05 / A few more details

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Something else on your mind?
hello@modelcraftsummit.com ↗

Who is Modelcraft Summit for?
Software, ML, and infrastructure engineers building, shipping, or operating AI systems—and the technical leaders supporting them. Expect practical, technical conversations. You don’t need a research background, just a real interest in making AI work beyond the demo.
What’s included in my ticket?
Your all-access pass includes every talk, the live failure-analysis session, the evaluation lab, small-group office hours, lunch, coffee, snacks, and the evening gathering. One ticket, the whole day. No session upgrades or add-ons.
Should I bring a laptop?
Yes, if you’d like to take part in the hands-on evaluation lab. We’ll provide sample model outputs, datasets, and instructions. A modern browser is all you need—no paid subscriptions, API credits, or special hardware required.
Can we register as a team?
Absolutely. Groups of 3 or more receive 15% off the current ticket price, automatically applied in registration. One person can register up to 10 attendees together.
What if my plans change?
Request a full refund through September 16, 2027. After that date, you can transfer your ticket to a colleague at no charge until October 9, 2027. Contact hello@modelcraftsummit.com with your registration details.
Is the venue accessible?
Morgan Manufacturing has step-free entry and accessible restrooms. We’ll have a quiet room available throughout the day, and lunch will include vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options. Email hello@modelcraftsummit.com by October 2, 2027 for specific access or dietary requests.

A BETTER ROOM FOR EVERYONE

Our code of conduct.

Modelcraft Summit is a place to learn openly, ask honest questions, and treat each other with respect.

  1. Be thoughtful and inclusive. Welcome different backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience. Harassment or discrimination is not tolerated.
  2. Challenge ideas, not people. Ask constructive questions, make room for other voices, and assume good intent.
  3. Respect privacy. Ask before photographing attendees or sharing details from private office-hour conversations.
  4. Ask for support. Speak with a staff member wearing a Modelcraft badge or contact hello@modelcraftsummit.com. Reports will be handled discreetly.

This code applies to every session, the lab, office hours, and the evening gathering. Organizers may remove attendees who violate it.
