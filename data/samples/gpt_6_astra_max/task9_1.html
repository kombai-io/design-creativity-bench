Who is Stackglass Live for?

Platform engineers, SREs, infrastructure engineers, developer experience leaders, and engineering managers building or operating production systems. Expect technical conversations and practical lessons, not a vendor expo.

Do I need to bring a laptop?

Yes, for the hands-on incident-response simulation. All exercise materials are included, and you’ll work in small teams. No special setup or software installation is needed in advance.

What if my plans change?

Tickets are refundable through September 16, 2027. After that, you can transfer your ticket to a teammate at no charge through October 13. Email hello@stackglass.live with your registration reference and we’ll help.

Is the venue accessible? What about dietary needs?

Morgan Manufacturing offers step-free access and accessible restrooms. Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free lunch options are available. Add access or dietary requests when you register so we can make your day a good one.

OUR SHARED AGREEMENT

Good company, by design.

Stackglass Live is a welcoming, respectful space for people of every background and experience level. This code applies to all sessions, office hours, and the evening gathering.

  • Be curious and generous. Challenge ideas, never a person’s identity or worth.
  • Respect boundaries. Ask before taking photographs, sharing private information, or recording a conversation.
  • Keep it welcoming. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and unwelcome advances are not accepted.
  • Make room for others. Listen, leave space for quieter voices, and avoid unsolicited sales pitches.

Need support? Speak with a staff member wearing a green organizer badge or email hello@stackglass.live. We’ll respond privately and help you feel safe. Organizers may remove anyone who violates this policy.
