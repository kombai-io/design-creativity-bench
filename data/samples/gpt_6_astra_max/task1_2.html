Can I change my plan later?

Absolutely. Move between Monitoring and Deployment as your needs evolve, or update your billing cycle in Workspace settings. You’ll always see the new price before confirming a change.

Are my AWS and Azure costs included?

Your plan covers Stratum’s monitoring and deployment tools. Compute, storage, and other cloud usage are billed separately by AWS or Azure, directly to your existing cloud accounts. Your infrastructure stays in your control.

How does yearly billing work?

Pay for 12 months upfront and save 20% on any plan. For example, Deployment for AWS + Azure is $2,880 per month with yearly billing, paid as $34,560 per year instead of $43,200.
