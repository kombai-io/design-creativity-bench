Before you jump in

A few good
questions.

We’re starting focused, and building with the teams who need a little less waiting.

What’s in the first beta?
We’re starting with OpenAPI-based REST APIs: spec imports, repository syncing, hosted mock endpoints, realistic responses and error scenarios, and shared environments. GraphQL and gRPC aren’t part of the initial beta.
Do I need a backend already running?
No. Your OpenAPI specification is the starting point. MorrowSpec creates a hosted mock from that contract, so your team can build and test before the real backend is available.
What happens when my API spec changes?
Your mock updates to reflect the underlying specification. Connect your code repository to keep the source spec synced, instead of manually rebuilding mock data every time your API evolves.
How will I get an invite?
We’ll invite teams in small batches so we can learn from their workflows and feedback. Join the waitlist with your work email, and we’ll let you know when your team can get started.
