Which plan is right for my store?

Choose Analytics if you want clear insights and recommendations you can act on yourself. Choose Optimization if you want Grove to continuously test and improve your store for you, with priority support along the way.

How does annual billing work?

Pay once for 12 months and save 20% compared with monthly billing. For example, Shopify Optimization is $1,600 per month on an annual plan, billed as $19,200 once a year.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. Manage your subscription in Billing settings anytime. You’ll see any billing adjustment before confirming a plan change. Canceling stops your next renewal; you keep access through the end of your paid billing period.

What does Both platforms include?

One plan for your Shopify and WooCommerce stores. Analytics is $1,800 per month and Optimization is $3,600 per month. That’s 10% less than two separate platform plans, and the additional 20% annual billing discount still applies.
