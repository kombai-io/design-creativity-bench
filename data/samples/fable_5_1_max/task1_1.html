Common questions

Which plan should I choose?

Choose Analytics if you want a clear picture of what's happening in your store and prefer to make changes yourself. Choose Optimization if you'd rather we run the experiments and apply the winning changes for you, automatically. Most growing stores pick Optimization so improvements ship without extra work from their team.

When will I be charged?

Not yet. On the next step you'll review your order and enter payment details. Your plan starts as soon as payment is confirmed — and it's covered by the 14-day money-back guarantee from that moment.

Can I switch plans or cancel later?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel anytime from your dashboard. When you switch, we prorate the difference so you never pay twice for the same period.

How does the 14-day money-back guarantee work?

If Storelift isn't a fit within your first 14 days, email support and we'll refund you in full — no questions asked. It applies to both monthly and annual plans.

Do I need a developer to set this up?

No. Your store is already connected from Store Setup. Analytics starts collecting data immediately, and Optimization changes are applied through your platform's official API — every change can be rolled back with one click.

What does “Both” include?

One subscription that covers your Shopify store and your WooCommerce store, with a single combined dashboard, for 10% less than two separate plans.

How does annual billing work?

You pay for 12 months upfront and save 20% compared with paying monthly. If you start on monthly billing, you can switch to annual at any time from your dashboard.
