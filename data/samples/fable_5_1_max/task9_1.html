Who it's for

Built for the teams behind the platform.

Stackglass Live is for the people who build the internal platforms other engineers depend on and keep production running when it counts. If your work sits underneath everyone else's, this day is for you.

Platform engineering

Teams designing and running internal platforms, paved paths, and the tooling the rest of engineering builds on.

Reliability & SRE

Engineers accountable for availability and incident response — and for making systems degrade gracefully instead of falling over.

Infrastructure

The people who own compute, networking, and delivery — the foundations everything else assumes will simply be there.

Developer experience

Anyone working to make shipping software faster, safer, and less frustrating for their whole organization.

Bringing colleagues? The hands-on simulation and small-group office hours are even better with the people you're on call with. Registration is per attendee, so each teammate registers individually.

The program

One day. Four ways to look inside real systems.

Everything on the program comes from practitioners working on systems they actually run — in formats that go deeper than a talk track alone.

01 · Talks

Practitioner talks

Engineers share what they've built and learned across internal platforms, reliability, infrastructure, and developer experience — with the specifics that only come from running the system yourself.

  • Internal platforms
  • Reliability
  • Infrastructure
  • Developer experience
02 · Live on stage

Live architecture teardown

Follow one real production system from its earliest version to today. We trace how the architecture changed as usage grew and the engineering organization scaled — the constraints behind each decision, the trade-offs accepted, and what the team would do differently now.

03 · Hands-on

Incident-response simulation

Step into a realistic production incident with other attendees. Triage, coordinate, and communicate under pressure, then debrief on what helped and what got in the way — the kind of practice most teams only get during the real thing.

FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

The essentials for deciding whether Stackglass Live is the right day for you and your team.

Is Stackglass Live in person only?

Yes. Stackglass Live is a one-day, in-person conference in Chicago on October 16, 2027. The teardown, the simulation, and office hours are all built around being in the room together.

Who should attend?

Platform engineering and reliability teams — including the engineers who build internal platforms, run infrastructure, own reliability and incident response, or work on developer experience. Engineering leaders responsible for those teams will find the office hours especially useful.

What does registration include?

All sessions — the practitioner talks, the live architecture teardown, the incident-response simulation, and office hours — plus lunch and the evening gathering.

What is the live architecture teardown?

A guided look at how a real production system evolved as its usage and engineering organization grew: what the architecture looked like at each stage, why it changed, and what those changes cost.

How does the incident-response simulation work?

It's hands-on. You'll work through a realistic incident alongside other attendees, then debrief together on what worked. Tick the box on the registration form if you'd like to take part.

How do office hours work?

Small groups sit down with speakers and experienced platform leaders for direct conversation. Come with specific questions about your systems, your team, or what you're planning next.

Can several people from my team register?

Yes. Registration is per attendee, so each colleague registers individually — and the simulation and office hours are richer with teammates in the room.
