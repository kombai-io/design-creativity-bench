We’re starting small, with a focused private beta and the tools webhook teams need most.

What’s included in the private beta?

The beta starts with HTTP-based webhooks and common signing methods. You’ll get temporary endpoints, event inspection and replay, editable payloads, shared debugging sessions, and generated code snippets.

HTTP webhooksCommon signing methodsTeam workspaces
Will this affect my production systems?

Patchbay gives you a separate workspace and temporary endpoints for testing before production. You choose the replay destination, so you can keep debugging in your local or staging environment without sending test events to live systems.

Who is Patchbay Cloud for?

Software teams building and maintaining webhook integrations. Engineers can test the tricky cases, while support can follow the same event history and debugging session instead of piecing together screenshots.

How do private-beta invitations work?

Join the waitlist with your email. We’ll invite teams in small batches as the beta grows and send you an update when your spot is ready. Your feedback will help shape what we build next.
