hello@stackglass.live

What does my ticket include?

Everything on the program: practitioner talks, the live architecture teardown, the incident-response simulation, and small-group office hours. Lunch, coffee, and the evening gathering are included too. It’s one ticket for the whole day.

Who is Stackglass Live for?

Platform engineers, SREs, infrastructure engineers, developer experience teams, and the leaders who support them. If you build internal tools, run production systems, or help engineering teams work better, you’ll be in good company.

Do I need to bring a laptop?

Yes, bring a laptop for the hands-on incident-response simulation. You’ll work in small teams using a browser-based training environment. No special software or production access is needed; setup instructions are shared before the event.

Can I register my whole team?

Absolutely. Register four or more people together and a 15% team discount is applied automatically. Each attendee gets the same all-access experience, and you can finalize attendee names after booking.

What if my plans change?

Full refunds are available through September 16, 2027. After that, you can transfer your ticket to a teammate until October 8, 2027. Contact hello@stackglass.live with your registration reference and we’ll help.

What about accessibility and dietary needs?

The venue has step-free entry, accessible restrooms, and seating throughout the event. Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free lunch options are available. Share any additional accommodations with hello@stackglass.live by October 2, 2027.
