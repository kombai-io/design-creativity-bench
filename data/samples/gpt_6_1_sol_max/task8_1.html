03 / Before you join

Good questions.
Straight answers.

We're starting small, with teams who want to shape a better way to build.

Which APIs will the private beta support?

The first beta is focused on OpenAPI-based REST APIs. Import an OpenAPI specification or sync one from a code repository to create your hosted mock environment.

How is this different from static mock data?

MorrowSpec uses your API contract to create realistic responses, including errors and test scenarios. Your team works against a shared, hosted endpoint instead of maintaining separate local fixtures.

What happens when my specification changes?

Your mock API updates when the underlying specification changes. Connect your repository to keep the environment in step with your latest API contract, without rebuilding your mocks by hand.

Who is the beta for, and how do I get access?

Teams building and testing before backend services are ready—across frontend, backend, QA, and product. Join the waitlist with your work email. We're inviting early teams in small batches to help shape MorrowSpec.
