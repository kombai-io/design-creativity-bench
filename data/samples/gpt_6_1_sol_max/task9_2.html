Say hello ↗
Who is Modelcraft Summit for? +

Engineers building, shipping, and operating AI in production: ML engineers, software engineers, infrastructure leads, and technical founders. You don’t need a particular stack, just some hands-on experience and a healthy curiosity.

What does my ticket include? +

Your all-access pass includes every practitioner talk, the live failure-analysis session, the hands-on evaluation lab, and small-group speaker office hours. Lunch, coffee, and our evening gathering are included too.

Do I need to bring a laptop? +

Yes, bring a charged laptop for the evaluation lab. We’ll provide sample model outputs, datasets, and guided exercises. The lab focuses on practical evaluation methods, so you can participate regardless of the tools you use at work.

Can I bring my team? +

Absolutely. Register 4 or more people together to receive 15% off automatically. You can book up to 8 tickets in one registration. For a larger group, email hello@modelcraftsummit.com and we’ll help coordinate your team.

What if my plans change? +

Tickets are fully refundable through September 16, 2027. After that, you can transfer your ticket to a colleague at no charge through October 9, 2027. Email us with your registration reference and we’ll take care of it.
