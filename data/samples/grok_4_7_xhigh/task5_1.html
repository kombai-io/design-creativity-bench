The ecosystem, placed

One basin, four layers.

PostgreSQL, Amazon S3, and Apache Kafka are inlets. Snowflake, Google BigQuery, and Databricks are warehouse landings. dbt transforms what has arrived. Looker reads the result. Select any system on the plate.

What connection makes possible

Four changes, none of them a new system of record.

The shores stay. What changes is the path between them — and the morning the rest of the stack can trust that path.

01

One movement layer

Point-to-point export jobs give way to named routes. PostgreSQL, Amazon S3, and Apache Kafka are adopted, not replaced.

02

Land where you already analyze

Snowflake, Google BigQuery, and Databricks remain the systems of record. One lake can deliver to more than one of them, as the sample account does.

03

Models that trust the arrival

dbt runs against loads Conduit Lake has confirmed. The commerce project’s 11 runs on 12 March each started from a landed route, not from a hopeful clock.

04

Explores with a path back

Looker stays the front door. Revenue, Fulfillment, and Inventory refreshes trace to a route, a landing, and the system that emitted the row.
