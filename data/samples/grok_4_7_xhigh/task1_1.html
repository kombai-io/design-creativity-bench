Questions that usually hold people here

Will I be charged on this page?

No. This page only saves the choice. The payment step collects a card or ACH and creates the invoice. How does annual billing work?

Every price on this page is a monthly rate. Annual billing multiplies that rate by 12 and takes 20% off, charged once a year. Shopify Optimization is $2,000 per month, or $1,600 per month ($19,200 for the year) — $4,800 less than paying monthly. Both Optimization is $3,600 per month, or $2,880 per month ($34,560 for the year). Switch the toggle before payment. The 20% is the standard annual term, not a coupon.

What is the difference between Analytics and Optimization?

Analytics reports. Optimization reports and runs the work: merchandising, price tests inside the ±8% guardrail set on March 12, checkout experiments, and reorder suggestions. Optimization includes 8 seats (you use 2) and Jordan Hale. Analytics includes 2 seats, which Maya Chen and Ellis Ward already fill, and does not auto-publish anything. The product is an optimization app — Analytics is the lighter tier if you only want the digest.

What does Both cover if WooCommerce is not connected?

Both (Shopify + WooCommerce) covers harborandpine.com immediately and one WooCommerce storefront you connect after payment. Analytics is $1,800/mo instead of $2,000. Optimization is $3,600/mo instead of $4,000. If you never add WooCommerce, you would be paying the bundle rate for a store you have not connected — choose Shopify if you only want Harbor & Pine.

Can I change plans or cancel?

Yes, from billing settings. Monthly plans stop at the end of the paid month. Annual plans are refundable in the first 14 days if Optima has not published a change; after that, unused months are credited on a prorated basis if you cancel. Upgrades are prorated. Downgrades apply on the next invoice. There is no revenue share and no per-order fee on any plan.
