How it fits

Your stack stays. The distance between systems does not.

LedgerNorth does not ask a finance team to migrate the books. It sits on the bus those systems already should have shared.

01

Place the books

Connect Oracle NetSuite, QuickBooks, Xero, or Sage Intacct. LedgerNorth reads entities, the chart, and open items, and can write coded journals back. The accounting system remains the system of record.

02

Attach the evidence

Plaid brings bank balances and transactions. Stripe brings payouts, fees, refunds, and disputes. Ramp brings cards, reimbursements, and bills. Workday brings workers, cost centers, and payroll journals.

03

Run one close

Matched activity is ready to post. What remains sits in an exception queue with an owner. A close calendar replaces the scavenger hunt across five logins.
