Before you pay

When will I be charged?

Not on this page. Payment is step 3. If you confirm today, Mar 20, 2026, an annual plan is charged once for 12 months. A monthly plan bills the first month today and again on Apr 20, 2026. Invoice KEEL-2026-0341 stays a draft until then.

Can I change coverage or module later?

Yes. Move between AWS, Azure, and Both, or between Monitoring and Deployment, any time. In the first 30 days the difference is prorated on the next invoice. After that, downgrades apply at the end of the current period. Renewal for the annual plan is Mar 20, 2027, and you can cancel that renewal any day before it.

Does Deployment include Monitoring?

No. They are separate modules so you only pay for what you run. Most dual-cloud teams start with Deployment, the module that ships changes, and add Monitoring once pipelines are steady. kv-meridian-prod is already drifting; Monitoring is the module that watches that continuously. Maya Chen can add the second module to the draft invoice before it is issued.

What does the 20% annual discount apply to?

The monthly price of the option you select. Both is already 10% below AWS and Azure purchased separately ($1,800 vs $2,000 for Monitoring, $3,600 vs $4,000 for Deployment). Annual billing takes another 20% off that list price. The discounts stack; they are not applied twice to the same line.

Can finance pay by invoice?

Yes. On the payment step choose card or ACH invoice, Net 30, for annual plans. Sam Okonkwo is already a billing viewer, so the draft is visible to FinOps. Bill-to is prefilled: Meridian Logistics, attn Sam Okonkwo, ap@meridianlogistics.example. A PO field is on the next step. USD. Tax, if any, is calculated there.
