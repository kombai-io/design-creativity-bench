How it fits

A cross-section of the stack you already run.

Conduit Lake is the channel in the middle. Sources stay systems of record. Warehouses stay systems of decision. Nothing in this picture has to be replaced for the path to run.

Source volumes on the cards are capture, not landings. Apache Kafka saw 9.6 billion events across all topics in 30 days; Conduit Lake moves subscribed topics only. Destination volumes match the chart: Snowflake 875 million, Google BigQuery 454 million, Databricks 292 million, trailing 14 days.
