Who it’s for

For engineers past the prototype

Modelcraft Summit is for people who ship AI features to real users and get paged when they misbehave. Every session is led by someone who has built or run the system they’re describing.

You’ll get the most out of it if you…

  • Ship or operate LLM or ML features that real customers depend on
  • Own evals, retrieval pipelines, inference infrastructure or agent tooling
  • Have watched a model pass testing and then behave differently in production
  • Lead a team deciding how to evaluate, deploy and monitor AI systems

It’s probably not the right fit if you…

  • Want an introduction to AI concepts — sessions assume production experience
  • Are looking for a vendor expo or product demos
  • Need to attend remotely — the summit is in person only

Hands-on evaluation lab · 2:00–4:00 pm · Lab room

Evaluate two model configurations and make the ship call

Work through a realistic evaluation from start to finish using sample model outputs and datasets. You’ll compare a production configuration with a candidate, write a rubric, check an automated judge against human labels, and decide whether the candidate is ready.

  1. Inspect

    Read 40 paired outputs side by side and tag the failure types you see.

    15 min
  2. Define

    Write a four-criterion rubric and label a 60-row calibration set by hand.

    25 min
  3. Automate

    Run an LLM judge over all 400 rows and measure its agreement with your labels.

    35 min
  4. Slice

    Break results down by ticket type and conversation length to find hidden regressions.

    25 min
  5. Decide

    Make a ship / don’t-ship recommendation and defend it to your table.

    20 min

    What does registration include?

    Every ticket includes all sessions — the 12 main-stage talks, the live failure analysis, the evaluation lab and office hours — plus lunch and the evening gathering. Lab seats and office-hour groups are limited and reserved during registration.

    Is there a livestream or virtual ticket?

    No. Modelcraft Summit is in person only. The 12 main-stage talks are recorded and shared with registered attendees within three weeks. The failure analysis, lab and office hours are not recorded, so people can speak candidly.

    How technical is the content?

    Sessions assume you’ve built or operated production software and have worked with LLM or ML systems. Speakers skip the fundamentals and focus on architectures, trade-offs, metrics and incidents from systems running today.

    What do I need for the evaluation lab?

    A laptop with a current browser. We provide a hosted Python notebook, anonymized sample datasets and model outputs, so there’s nothing to install and no API keys or GPU are needed. Basic Python is helpful but not required. You can work in pairs.

    How do office hours work?

    There are two rounds with eight groups each. Every group has one host and up to eight attendees, and runs for 35–40 minutes without slides. Choose one group per round when you register and change your picks until October 9. Full groups have a waitlist.

    Can I get a refund or transfer my ticket?

    Yes. You get a full refund if you cancel by September 16, 2027. After that, you can transfer your ticket to a colleague for free until October 14, 2027, from the link in your confirmation email.

    Can my company pay by invoice?

    Yes, for team registrations of five or more. We issue net-30 invoices and can provide a W-9 and fill in vendor forms. Individual registrations are paid by card.

    Is there a code of conduct?

    Yes. All attendees, speakers, hosts and staff agree to it. It prohibits harassment in any form and applies throughout the day, including the evening gathering. Trained staff wear orange lanyards and can be reached on site or at safety@modelcraftsummit.com.
