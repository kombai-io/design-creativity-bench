LedgerNorth sits between your general ledger and the systems that feed it: bank accounts, payment processing, card spend, and payroll. Transactions arrive matched, coded, and reconciled, so close starts with clean books instead of CSV exports.

  • Two-way ledger syncEntries post to your ERP. Accounts, vendors, and dimensions sync back.
  • Read-only bank accessBank data arrives through Plaid. LedgerNorth can see balances but can't move money.
  • Mapping your controller can ownMap fields and GL accounts in a guided setup. No engineering ticket needed.

What becomes possible

5 of 6 workflows unlocked
  • St+Pl Unlocked
    Payouts matched to deposits

    Every Stripe payout is tied to its bank deposit, with fees and refunds split to the right accounts in NetSuite.

  • Pl Unlocked
    Daily bank reconciliation

    Balances and transactions from each account reconcile against NetSuite every morning instead of at month-end.

  • Ra Unlocked
    Card spend coded before close

    Ramp transactions arrive with receipts attached and coding mapped to your NetSuite chart of accounts.

  • Wd Unlocked
    Payroll journals by department

    Workday pay runs post as summarized entries split by department and entity, with no re-keying from reports.

  • Pl+Ra Unlocked
    Cash next to committed spend

    Bank balances sit beside pending card spend, so your cash forecast reflects money that's already committed.

  • St Unlocked
    Revenue and AR from billing data

    Charges, refunds, and disputes become revenue and receivable entries in NetSuite automatically.
