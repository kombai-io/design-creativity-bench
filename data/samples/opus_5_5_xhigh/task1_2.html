Common questions

Will I be charged when I click “Continue to payment”?

No. On the next step you'll add a payment method and see a final review screen. Nothing is charged until you click “Confirm & pay”.

Can I switch plans or clouds later?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or add a cloud anytime from Settings → Billing. Changes are prorated to the day, so you only pay for what you use.

How does the 20% annual discount work?

Annual plans are billed once per year at 20% off the monthly price. For example, Deployment on AWS drops from $2,000 to $1,600 per month, billed $19,200 per year.

What's the difference between Monitoring and Deployment?

Monitoring gives you metrics, logs, alerts and uptime checks for your cloud. Deployment adds automated CI/CD, safe release strategies and rollbacks, and includes everything in Monitoring.

Is there a refund policy?

Every plan comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee. If Skyforge isn't a fit, contact support within 14 days for a full refund.
