Common questions

When will I be charged?

Not yet. On the next step, you'll review your order and enter your payment details. You're only charged after you confirm there.

Can I switch plans or platforms later?

Yes. Upgrades start right away and are prorated to the day. Downgrades start at the beginning of your next billing period. You can also add WooCommerce or Shopify later and move to the bundle.

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are 20% cheaper than paying monthly, and you pay for the year upfront. For example, Optimization for Shopify costs $19,200 a year instead of $24,000.

What does the Shopify + WooCommerce bundle include?

One subscription covers one Shopify store and one WooCommerce store. You get a single dashboard and reports across both. It costs 10% less than two separate plans: $1,800 instead of $2,000 for Analytics, and $3,600 instead of $4,000 for Optimization, per month.

How do I cancel?

Go to Settings → Billing and cancel in one click. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. You don't need to contact anyone.
