How it works

One master cut. Every market, ready to publish.

Upload your finished video once. SceneLilt transcribes it, translates it, dubs it and adapts the on-screen text for each market. Your team then fine-tunes, reviews and exports every version in one shared workspace.

The screens below follow one example project: Northpeak Outdoor’s “Summer Trail” 30-second hero spot, localized for 6 markets.

  1. 01Set upAbout 2 minutes

    Upload your master and choose your markets

    Drop in your locked cut as MOV, MP4 or ProRes, up to 4K. Then pick the markets you’re launching in and the language for each, so Mexico gets Latin American Spanish and Spain gets Castilian. SceneLilt creates a separate version for every market.

    Your team
    Uploads the master video and selects markets and languages.
    SceneLilt
    Checks the file and sets up one version per market.
  2. 02AnalyzeAutomatic · about 6 minutes

    SceneLilt transcribes the dialogue and finds all spoken and on-screen content

    The original dialogue is transcribed with speaker labels and timecodes. Every frame is then scanned for on-screen text, including supers, lower thirds, legal lines and end cards, so nothing is left untranslated. Brand names and logos are flagged so they stay unchanged.

    Your team
    Skims the transcript and corrects anything the audio made unclear.
    SceneLilt
    Transcribes the dialogue and identifies all spoken and on-screen content.
  3. 03LocalizeAutomatic · ~40 min for 6 markets

    Translate, dub and adapt on-screen text for each market

    Each line is translated for its market, then voiced as dubbed audio that follows the original speaker’s timing and delivery. On-screen text is re-set in the frame in the target language, using your fonts and layout. Switch markets to see the same line in each version.

    Your team
    Adds glossary terms and brand-voice notes once, if you have them.
    SceneLilt
    Translates, generates dubbed audio and adapts on-screen text.
  4. 04RefineAt your team’s pace

    Compare with the original and fine-tune every line

    Play any localized version next to the original, frame for frame. Change the wording, adjust timing to land on a cut, fix how a product name is pronounced or change the tone of a read. Then regenerate just that line, not the whole video.

    Your team
    Edits language, timing, pronunciation and tone where needed.
    SceneLilt
    Re-renders only the lines you change and saves every version.
  5. 05ApproveEach market in parallel

    Reviewers comment on and approve each market

    Send regional teams, legal and brand stakeholders a review link to their market’s version. They leave comments pinned to the exact timecode and approve the version when it’s ready. Each market moves at its own pace, so France’s notes never hold up Germany’s launch.

    Reviewers
    Comment on and approve the version for their own market.
    SceneLilt
    Tracks every comment, change and approval for each market.
  6. 06DeliverMinutes per format

    Export approved versions in the formats you publish

    As soon as a market is approved, export it in the specs each channel needs, from a broadcast master to 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 social cuts, caption files and audio stems. Download the files, share a link or send them straight to your DAM.

    Your team
    Chooses the formats and where the files should go.
    SceneLilt
    Renders every approved market in every format you selected.

Your workflow

SceneLilt fits between your final edit and your launch

SceneLilt starts once your edit is locked and finishes when the files are ready to publish. Your editors, agencies and channels stay the same. The translation, dubbing, text versioning and approval rounds in between move into one workspace.
