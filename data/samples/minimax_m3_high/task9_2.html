01 — About the summit

Built for the engineers who get paged at 2 AM.

Most AI conferences optimize for research novelty. Modelcraft Summit optimizes for the rest: evals that don't lie, retrieval that doesn't drift, inference budgets that don't explode, and the unglamorous work of making systems reliable at scale.

Modelcraft Summit is a one-day, single-track conference for engineers who design, build, evaluate, and operate AI features inside real products. The program is curated from submissions — not sponsorships — and every talk is delivered by someone who has actually shipped the thing they're describing.

There are no vendor keynotes, no "thought leader" panels, and no sales-floor interludes. You will spend the day with 420 practitioners who are wrestling with the same problems you are: how to evaluate a model you can't fully specify, how to keep retrieval fast as the corpus grows, how to debug an agent that does the wrong thing in a way you didn't anticipate.

"The most useful day of the year for our infra team. We came back with three concrete fixes we shipped the following week." — Priya Ramaswamy, Staff Engineer, Notion (2026 attendee)

Chicago in mid-October is reliably crisp, the venue is a 12-minute walk from the river, and the program wraps with an evening reception that runs until the staff gently asks you to leave.

02 — Program tracks

Five tracks. One focused day.

The 2027 program is organized into five overlapping tracks that map to how AI systems actually break in production. Every session is tagged to at least one.

01

Model Evaluation

Designing evals that fail for the right reasons — and survive contact with production traffic.

  • Offline vs online
  • LLM-as-judge
  • Drift detection
  • Annotator design
02

Retrieval Systems

Indexing pipelines, hybrid search, re-ranking, and the long tail of "why is this not in the results."

  • Vector + BM25
  • Rerankers
  • Freshness
  • Latency budgets
03

Inference

Throughput, cost, and reliability under real workloads — batching, caching, and the GPU supply chain.

  • vLLM · Triton
  • Speculative
  • Quantization
  • Multi-region
04

Agents

Tool use, planning, and the operational reality of letting a model call your APIs unsupervised.

  • Tool protocols
  • Sandboxing
  • Long horizons
  • Cost control
05

Production Reliability

Observability, rollback, guardrails, and the human processes that catch what dashboards don't.

  • Tracing
  • Shadowing
  • Incident review
  • SLOs for LLMs

★ Flagship 10:30 – 11:15·Main Hall·Live failure analysis

When the eval said 94%: a live teardown of a model that passed every test and broke in production.

An open-source RAG system scored 94.1% on the team's offline eval suite and shipped to 11M users. Within six weeks, the on-call rotation was paged 14 times for hallucinated citations. In this live session, the engineer who built it walks through the original eval design, replays three of the production failures in front of the audience, and rebuilds the eval harness from scratch in 25 minutes — alongside the model that broke it.

10 — Questions

Frequently asked.

If your question isn't answered below, write to hello@modelcraftsummit.com. We reply within one business day, usually from a human named Mara.

Is Modelcraft Summit recorded?

The main-hall talks and the live failure analysis are recorded and posted to all ticket holders within 48 hours. Office hours are not recorded, on purpose — they only work if people can ask the question they'd actually ask. The hands-on lab is recorded as a screencast of the instructor's terminal; attendees' terminals are not.

Will talks be livestreamed?

No. Modelcraft Summit is intentionally in-person, and the program is designed around that — including the live failure analysis, which depends on the audience being in the room. Recordings replace livestreaming.

Can I expense this?

Yes — the program is a continuing-education conference for engineering professionals, and we provide a paid invoice and a one-page justification letter on request. Most attendees expense it under professional development; some under team off-sites.

What's the refund policy?

Full refund up to 30 days before the event (Sept 16, 2027). 50% refund from 30 to 14 days before. No refunds within 14 days of the event, but tickets are transferable to a colleague at no cost.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes. The short version: be excellent to each other, don't record other attendees without permission, and leave the sales pitch at home. The long version is on the registration confirmation page and at the venue.

I'm a vendor — can I sponsor?

Sponsorship slots are limited and sold separately from tickets. Sponsors get a single table at the reception and a logo on the printed program; they do not get speaking slots, badge scanners, or email access to attendees. Sponsorship inquiries: sponsors@modelcraftsummit.com.

What if I'm not an "AI engineer"?

The program is built for engineers building and operating production AI systems, but it's useful to a wider audience — SREs, platform engineers, technical PMs, and engineering managers whose teams ship AI features. About 12% of attendees are not hands-on engineers; they're welcome.

Can I bring a guest to the reception?

Reception +1 passes are available for $85 during registration, subject to a cap. The reception is on the river-view terrace and capacity is limited by the fire code, not the budget.
