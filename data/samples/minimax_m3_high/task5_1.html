What becomes possible

Once your systems are connected, pipelines stop being the bottleneck.

Conduit Lake handles the undifferentiated work of moving data — so your team can ship the work that actually moves the business.

Real-time CDC replication

Capture row-level changes from Postgres, MySQL, MongoDB, Oracle, and SQL Server in milliseconds — without fragile triggers or batch windows.

Median lag · production 312ms p99

Schema that evolves with you

When upstream columns change, downstream tables, dbt models, and BI dashboards update automatically — with safe expand-and-contract migrations.

Auto-handled since launch 2.1M migrations

Reverse ETL, built in

Sync curated segments and metrics from your warehouse back into the SaaS tools your revenue and growth teams already live in — no separate vendor.

Destinations supported 31 SaaS apps

Observability on every row

Lineage, row counts, schema diffs, and replayable history for every pipeline — surfaced alongside your warehouse, not in a separate tool.

Rows tracked to date 14.2B / day

Compliance, by default

PII masking, column-level encryption, and regional data residency controls ship on every connector — SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, and GDPR ready.

Frameworks covered SOC 2 · HIPAA · GDPR

Python-first transforms

Apply type-aware transformations, enrichments, and custom logic in Python — versioned with your data, observable in the same UI as the pipeline.
