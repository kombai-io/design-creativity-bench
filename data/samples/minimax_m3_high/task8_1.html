FAQ

Questions teams ask before joining.

If yours isn't here, ping us at hello@morrowspec.dev.

Q What spec formats are supported?

OpenAPI 3.0 and 3.1, in JSON or YAML. We resolve all $ref shapes, including circular references and oneOf/anyOf unions. Swagger 2.0 imports with a one-click upgrade.

Q How do teams share environments?

Invite by email or org. Each person gets a personal access token scoped to a specific environment. Environments are isolated — frontend staging can't accidentally hit QA's seeded data.

Q What happens after the private beta?

Beta users get grandfathered pricing for 12 months from GA. The free tier stays generous for small teams and side projects — we don't want price to be a reason to roll your own mock server again.

Q Can I self-host MorrowSpec?

Not yet. A self-hosted edition is on the Q2 2026 roadmap. For now, every mock is hosted in your choice of AWS us-east-1, eu-west-1 or ap-southeast-2, with at-rest encryption and SOC 2 Type II in progress.

Q How is my spec data handled?

We never store response payloads from your mock — only the spec itself and request metadata. Specs are encrypted at rest and in transit, and you can delete your workspace and all data with a single API call.

Q When will GraphQL, gRPC and AsyncAPI be supported?

GraphQL is on the Q1 2026 roadmap. AsyncAPI lands shortly after for webhook-heavy APIs. gRPC is further out — it's a different architecture and we want to do it right rather than rush it.
