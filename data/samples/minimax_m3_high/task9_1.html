01 · The program

Practitioner talks, not vendor pitches.

Twenty-eight speakers across one day. Every talk is delivered by someone who maintains the system they describe — staff engineers, principal SREs, platform leads, and directors who've had the on-call pager.

03 · The teardown

One system. Seven years. Five rewrites.

Reza Aslani walks through the real production architecture of a checkout pipeline — from a single Postgres + cron in 2020 to a multi-region, event-driven service running in 2027 — with the org chart and incident history behind every decision.

Save a seat · $489 →
Main hall · 11:00 – 12:15

The architecture teardown

This is the talk we wish we'd had five years ago. Reza shares the actual production diagram of a system serving 4.2B requests a month — including the dead ends, the rewrites that were rolled back in a week, and the one decision he'd reverse if he could.

  • 2020: single Postgres, single cron, 12 engineers, 200 RPS
  • 2022: queue-first split, first rewrite, 60 engineers, 4K RPS
  • 2024: event-driven services, the rewrite that almost killed the team
  • 2026: multi-region active-active, the org chart that made it possible
  • 2027: where it breaks, what's still hard, and what's next

Slides and the full postmortem archive are emailed to attendees 48 hours after the conference.

07 · Common questions

FAQ.

If you have a question that's not answered here, email hello@stackglass.live — we usually reply within a day.

Will sessions be recorded?

Yes. Every main-hall talk and the architecture teardown are professionally recorded. Attendees get the full video library within two weeks of the event, and a public cut is released six weeks later on our YouTube channel.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes — and we take it seriously. Our code of conduct is published at stackglass.live/code and is enforced by a dedicated, trained response team on-site. By registering, you agree to it.

Can I get a refund?

Full refunds are available until September 18, 2027. After that, you can transfer your ticket to a colleague at no charge. Email us and we'll handle it within 24 hours.

What if I need to travel with a colleague?

The Team Pack ($429/seat at 4+) gets you a reserved lunch table, priority office-hour booking, and a single net-30 invoice. Larger groups (10+) get a 15% discount and a private 30-min Q&A with one speaker of your choice.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes — the Drake is fully ADA-compliant, with accessible rooms on every floor we use. Live captioning is provided on the main-hall stage. Email us at least 7 days in advance if you have specific access needs and we'll arrange them.

I'm a student / early in my career — should I come?

If you're working on infra, on-call, or building internal tooling, yes. The Community ticket ($129) and the mentorship lunch table exist specifically for you. We also have 12 travel stipends — applications close September 1.
