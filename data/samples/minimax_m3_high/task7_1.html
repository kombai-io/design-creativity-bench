Things teams ask before they plug us in.

The four questions that come up in every security review.

Does my data leave our AWS account?

No. Branchglass runs as an ECS service inside a VPC you control. The control plane only sees metadata — repo names, PR numbers, deployment status. Your database, secrets, and traffic never leave your account.

How are secrets handled per environment?

Secrets are pulled from your existing AWS Secrets Manager on every provision. We generate unique, scoped values for things like database passwords and never persist them outside your account.

What about cost — won't this be expensive?

Most teams spend less than $0.50 per PR lifecycle. We use spot capacity, scale-to-zero between pushes, and aggressive teardown. Branchglass itself is billed per active preview-hour, with generous free tiers for OSS and small teams.

Can I bring my own infrastructure module?

Yes. Point module: ./infra/preview at any Terraform, Pulumi, or CloudFormation template. Branchglass provisions and tears it down; you own the shape.

What if a reviewer wants production data?

By default, no. Synthetic fixtures only. If your compliance team signs off, we support masked snapshots — PII replaced at the column level — with audit logs of every query.

How long do environments live?

As long as the PR is open. You can also set a ttl safety net (default 72h) and pause destruction with a /keep comment on the PR.
