● BEFORE YOU ASK

Questions teams ask on day one.

Everything below is covered in depth in the walkthrough — but these five come up on every first call.

No. SceneLilt can match a reference voice from your master, or you can upload takes from your own voice talent and sync them to the cue sheet. Generated or recorded, timing and lip-flow are tuned the same way.

Each workspace carries glossaries and translation memories per market. Locked terms — product names, claims, legal lines — are flagged in the transcript and can't drift between versions.

SceneLilt detects on-screen text during transcription and re-typesets it inside each market's safe area — character limits, right-to-left scripts and number formats included. You can override any line by hand.

You assign approvers per market — in-country managers, brand leads, legal. Each sign-off is stamped to the version with a full comment history, so everyone knows what shipped and who signed it.

It sits between them. Your team keeps creative control of the master; SceneLilt removes the mechanical round-trips. Exports include caption sidecars and project XML so post work can continue in your usual tools.
