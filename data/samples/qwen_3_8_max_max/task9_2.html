01

Why Modelcraft

The demo works. Production is the exam.

Most AI conferences celebrate what models can do. Modelcraft Summit is about what happens after launch — the drift, the edge cases, the 2 a.m. pages, and the engineering that keeps systems honest.

Every session is led by a practitioner who runs these systems for real. You’ll hear how teams design evaluations they actually trust, keep retrieval quality from collapsing, control inference cost under live traffic, ship agents safely, and budget for reliability. Then you’ll tear a failure apart and build an eval of your own.
