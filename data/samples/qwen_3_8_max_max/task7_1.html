How it works

Every pull request gets its own production.

Branchglass sits between your repository and your cloud account. Open a pull request and an isolated, production-like environment appears with a link your whole team can test — every commit redeploys in place, and merging tears it all down.

What you can count on

Defaults engineered for real review.

six defaults · zero config required
01

Isolated by construction

Every PR gets dedicated compute, its own database seed, and its own secrets vault. Two pull requests never share state.

02

Production-like, not production

Same images, config, feature flags, and topology as prod — pointed at safe data. What you review is what ships.

03

Safe test data

Rows are synthetic or masked at the source. Real PII never enters a preview environment, and secrets are stubbed, never copied.

04

Ephemeral on purpose

Merge and close hooks plus hard TTLs guarantee teardown. An environment can outlive its PR only if you explicitly pin it.

05

Cost stays capped

Per-environment budgets, idle detection, and spot-friendly scheduling keep previews from becoming a line item.

06

Zero new YAML

No pipeline files committed to the repo. Defaults live in one config surface your platform team controls.
