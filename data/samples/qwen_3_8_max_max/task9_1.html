01 — THE BRIEF

Built for the people who run the stack.

SGL/27

Most conferences talk about platforms. Stackglass Live talks about what it actually took to build and keep one alive — the migrations, the outages, the org charts, the trade-offs. Every session is led by a practitioner who has shipped the thing they're describing.

It's deliberately small and deliberately in-person: 400 seats, one day, one room deep. You'll hear the talks, take apart a real architecture on a whiteboard, run an incident as a team, and sit eight-to-a-table with the speakers afterward.

Your ticket covers everything on the day — all sessions, lunch, and the evening gathering. You show up; we handle the rest.

03 — THE EXPERIENCE

Four ways in. Zero filler.

Every element of the day is built for people who operate real systems — listen, dissect, rehearse, and ask.

A //PRACTITIONER TALKS

Sixteen talks. Zero sales pitches.

Every session covers internal platforms, reliability, infrastructure, or developer experience — presented by engineers who built the thing on the slide. Expect postmortem-grade detail: numbers, failure modes, and the decisions made under pressure.

  • INTERNAL PLATFORMS & DEVELOPER EXPERIENCE
  • RELIABILITY, SLOs & INCIDENT PRACTICE
  • INFRASTRUCTURE AT REAL SCALE
  • EVERY TALK ENDS WITH REAL Q&A TIME

B //LIVE ARCHITECTURE TEARDOWN

One real system, taken apart on stage.

Not slides — a whiteboard. Follow how a genuine production system evolved decision by decision as its usage grew and its engineering organization grew with it: what split, what merged, what got thrown away, and why each move made sense at the time.

  • A REAL PRODUCTION SYSTEM, END TO END
  • EVOLUTION UNDER USAGE & ORG GROWTH
  • REJECTED ALTERNATIVES, DISCUSSED OPENLY

C //HANDS-ON INCIDENT SIMULATION

When the pager goes off, you'll have done this before.

Join a team in a facilitated incident-response simulation. Injects arrive the way real incidents do — ambiguous, overlapping, and time-boxed. You'll take roles, run comms, and resolve; then debrief blamelessly on what the system and the org each told you.

  • TEAM-BASED, FACILITATOR-RUN GAMEDAY
  • REALISTIC INJECTS, ESCALATING TIMELINE
  • BLAMELESS DEBRIEF + TAKE-HOME PLAYBOOK

D //SMALL-GROUP OFFICE HOURS

Direct access, not hallway luck.

Skip the corridor scramble. Office hours seat you eight-to-a-table with speakers and experienced platform leaders for focused 45-minute rotations. Bring the problem you've been carrying for a quarter; leave with options.

  • TABLES OF EIGHT · TWO ROTATIONS EACH
  • SPEAKERS + SEASONED PLATFORM LEADERS
  • PICK YOUR TOPICS AT BADGE PICKUP
