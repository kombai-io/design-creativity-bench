Transcribe & detect

SceneLilt hears everything your audience sees

Dialogue is transcribed with speaker separation, and everything non-spoken is flagged for a translator: on-screen titles, lower thirds, product callouts, price overlays and burned-in legal lines. Music and effects stay out of the dub script.

  • Speaker separation across 6 speakers, timestamped to the frame
  • On-screen text OCR — 12 strings detected with position and duration
  • Music, SFX and silence markers for editors
