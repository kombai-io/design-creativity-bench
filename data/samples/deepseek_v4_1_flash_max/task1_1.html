Billing questions, answered

Still unsure? Our team replies in under 4 minutes during business hours.

Can I change plans later?

Yes — upgrade, downgrade or move between Shopify, WooCommerce and Both at any time from your billing page. Changes are prorated to the day, so you only pay for what you use.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Every plan starts with 14 days free. We don't ask for a card up front, and you can cancel during the trial without being charged. If you continue past day 14, your first invoice is for the plan you selected.

How much do I save with annual billing?

Annual billing takes 20% off the monthly rate. Shopify and WooCommerce Analytics drops to $800/mo (billed $9,600 yearly) and Optimization to $1,600/mo (billed $19,200 yearly). The Both plan saves $8,640 a year on Optimization.

Does the Shopify plan cover my WooCommerce store?

No — each plan covers the platform it names. If you run both, choose Shopify + WooCommerce: it's $1,800/mo for Analytics and $3,600/mo for Optimization, which is $200–$400/mo cheaper than two separate subscriptions.

Is there a setup or onboarding fee?

Never. Connecting your store takes about three minutes, our team runs the first import for you, and your first Snapshot report is ready within 24 hours of going live.

What if it doesn't work for my store?

You're covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee on top of the free trial. Email billing@opticart.io within 30 days of your first invoice and we refund it in full — no forms, no exit interview.
