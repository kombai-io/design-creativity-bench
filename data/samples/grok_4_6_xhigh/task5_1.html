What becomes possible when the stack is connected

The value is not another tool to learn. It is a continuous path from the systems that run the business to the systems that explain it.

01 — Keep

Operational systems stay operational.

PostgreSQL, Amazon S3, and Apache Kafka remain where they are. Conduit Lake reads from them, so you never trade running the business for analyzing it.

02 — Land

Warehouses receive current data, not another export.

Snowflake, Google BigQuery, and Databricks become destinations the lake keeps aligned. Analysts query the business as it stands, not last week’s file.

03 — Compound

Models and dashboards share the same current.

dbt builds on tables Conduit Lake has already moved. Looker explores those same tables. The record, the model, and the metric stop drifting apart.
