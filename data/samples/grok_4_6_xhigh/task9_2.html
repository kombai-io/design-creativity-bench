Who this is for

The room after the demo.

Modelcraft Summit is for people who ship models past the prototype: evaluation, retrieval, inference, agents, and the reliability work that keeps them in production.

  • 01Engineers owning model quality, eval harnesses, and release gates.
  • 02Teams running retrieval systems that have to stay accurate under real traffic.
  • 03Platform and inference engineers accountable for latency, cost, and uptime.
  • 04Builders of agents who need production-grade control, not another demo loop.

Program

A working day, not a keynote circuit.

Every session is built for practitioners. You leave with methods you can take back to a running system — not a slide recap of the future.

Featured session

Live failure-analysis

A working session on an AI application that behaved one way in testing and another in production. We walk the gap — data, prompts, retrieval, evaluation, and operations — and name what the tests failed to catch.
