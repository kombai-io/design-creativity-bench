01

A close that inherits the truth

Journals, bank recs, payouts, and coded spend arrive already mapped. The close stops being a collection exercise and becomes a review of what the stack already knows.

02

Cash you can defend in a meeting

Plaid balances and Stripe payouts sit next to the GL, on the same clock. Treasury and accounting argue less because they are no longer holding two versions of the week.

03

Spend that posts itself

Ramp charges and bills land approved and coded to the account they belong to. Expense review happens before the variance, not in the last four days of the month.

04

Workforce cost inside the forecast

Workday headcount and compensation update actuals and the plan when the org changes — not as a quarter-end spreadsheet that never quite ties.
