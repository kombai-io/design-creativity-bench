Why this room exists

Built for the people who build the platform.

Internal platforms are how engineering organizations scale. Reliability is how they stay standing. Stackglass Live gathers practitioners for one focused day in Chicago—talks from the field, a live look at a production architecture, a hands-on incident, and small-group time with people who have already walked the path.

If you design golden paths, run infrastructure, own developer experience, or carry the pager when the platform bends, this day is for you. It is not a vendor showcase. It is a working conference for teams who have to ship the platform and keep it up.

Program

Four ways the day works.

The program is built so you leave with practice, not only slides: practitioner talks, a live architecture teardown, an incident-response simulation, and office hours with speakers and platform leaders.

01 — Featured

Live architecture teardown

A real production system, taken apart in the room. Follow how the architecture evolved as usage grew and the engineering organization changed with it—the tradeoffs that stuck, the ones that did not, and what the next layer had to absorb.

02 — Talks

Practitioner talks

Sessions on internal platforms, reliability, infrastructure, and developer experience. Grounded in production work: what scaled, what failed, and how teams changed the system without stopping the org.

03 — Hands-on

Incident-response simulation

Join a facilitated incident and practice the coordination that actually matters: signal, ownership, communication, and the decisions you make when the platform is the incident.

04 — Access

Small-group office hours

Direct time with speakers and experienced platform leaders. Bring the constraint you are in—migration, adoption, reliability targets, or the next platform bet—and work it in a small group.
