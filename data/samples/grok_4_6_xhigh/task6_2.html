01 — Fit

It does not become your ERP.

Oracle NetSuite, QuickBooks, Xero, and Sage Intacct remain the system of record. LedgerNorth syncs journals, entities, and dimensions so operations never drift from the books.

02 — Relevance

The ecosystem is the product.

Finance does not live in one vendor. The value is a durable map across GL, banks, processors, spend, and HCM—so every close, forecast, and control uses the same numbers.

What becomes possible

Once the graph is live, close, cash, spend, and people costs stop living in separate rooms.

01

A continuous close

Entries, bank recs, and intercompany flows stay aligned with NetSuite, QuickBooks, Xero, or Sage Intacct. Period-end becomes control, not archaeology.

02

Cash as a live position

Plaid balances and Stripe settlements compose one treasury picture—updated as money moves, not when someone refreshes a workbook.

03

Spend that posts itself

Ramp cards, bills, and receipts arrive pre-coded and matched to the ledger—governed at the source, audit-ready at close.

04

People costs in the P&L

Workday headcount and payroll land beside revenue and spend. Workforce actuals stop being a sidecar spreadsheet.
