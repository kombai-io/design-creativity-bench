Questions merchants ask before paying

What is the difference between Analytics and Optimization?

Analytics shows you what is happening — traffic, conversion, product, and cohort reporting. Optimization includes Analytics and then runs experiments and automated merchandising so the store actually improves without a weekly agency retainers.

How does annual billing work?

Annual billing is 20% off the monthly rate, charged once per year. Shopify Analytics is $1,000/mo or $9,600/year; Optimization is $2,000/mo or $19,200/year. Both-platform Analytics is $1,800/mo or $17,280/year; Both Optimization is $3,600/mo or $34,560/year.

Can I start with one platform and add the other later?

Yes. Upgrade from Shopify or WooCommerce to Both at any time. We prorate the difference so you are not double-charged for unused days.

Is there a setup fee or long contract?

No setup fee. Monthly is month-to-month. Annual is prepaid for twelve months and can be cancelled before the next renewal. You can move between Analytics and Optimization on the next billing date.
