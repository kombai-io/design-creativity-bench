The gap

Webhook debugging is still tribal knowledge.

Events fire once, payloads live in screenshots, and failure paths get tested against real customers. Patchbay is the shared workspace for webhook truth.

WITHOUT PATCHBAY

Guess, ping vendors, ship anyway

Someone tunnels a laptop, pastes a payload into Slack, and asks the provider to resend. Timeouts, retries, and signature mismatches are discovered in production logs.

WITH PATCHBAY

Capture once, rehearse everything

A durable session records every request. Engineering replays and mutates events. Support inspects the same history. Handlers are generated from real payloads, not docs.

Product

Built for the full integration loop.

From first handshake to failure drills, Patchbay Cloud keeps webhook work inspectable, repeatable, and shareable across the team.

Temporary endpoints

Spin up unique HTTPS URLs that receive webhook events instantly. Inspect headers, body, timing, and signatures as they arrive—without opening production ports.

Replay, mutate, break

Resend captured events as-is or edit the payload first. Rehearse timeouts, 4xx/5xx responses, and malformed signatures without waiting on a vendor or a customer.

Shared event history

Invite engineering and support into the same session. Pin failures, annotate payloads, and hand off a live history instead of a screenshot thread.

Code from captured events

Generate starter snippets that verify signatures and handle the payload you actually received—ready to drop into the languages your team already ships.

Workflow

Four steps from empty port to proven handler.

Patchbay stays out of production traffic. It is the rehearsal space you use before you wire a provider to live systems.

Create an endpoint

Mint a temporary URL and point any HTTP webhook provider at it. Common HMAC signing methods are ready on day one of beta.

Capture the event

Every request is recorded: method, headers, body, timing, and signature check. Nothing disappears after the first delivery.

Replay and fail safely

Mutate payloads, force error paths, and confirm retries. Share the session so support sees the same evidence engineering does.

Generate and graduate

Export handler snippets from the captured event, then connect production only after the integration behaves as designed.

Who it’s for

Software teams who ship integrations, not just features.
