What is Modelcraft Summit?

A one-day, ticketed, in-person conference for engineers building and operating production AI systems. It takes place in Chicago on October 16, 2027.

Is this a hands-on event?

Yes. Alongside practitioner talks, the program includes a hands-on evaluation lab using sample model outputs and datasets, plus an interactive live failure-analysis session.

Will I get time with speakers?

Yes. Small-group office hours are designed for direct access to speakers and experienced AI engineering leaders. Bring a specific question or compare notes on a system you are operating.

What does registration include?

Your ticket includes all sessions, participation in the evaluation lab and office hours, lunch, and the evening gathering.

Where in Chicago is the summit?

The summit is in Chicago, Illinois. Detailed venue and arrival information will be provided to registered attendees.
