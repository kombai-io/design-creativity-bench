05 Need to know

Before you book.

The essentials for deciding whether Stackglass Live belongs on your calendar.

What is Stackglass Live?

Stackglass Live is a one-day, ticketed conference for platform engineering and reliability teams. It brings practitioner talks, a live system teardown, a hands-on incident simulation, and small-group office hours together in one in-person program.

When and where is it?

Stackglass Live takes place in person in Chicago on Saturday, October 16, 2027.

Who will get the most from the day?

Platform engineers, SRE and reliability practitioners, infrastructure teams, developer experience teams, and engineering leaders responsible for the systems and practices behind software delivery.

Is the incident-response session participatory?

Yes. It is a hands-on simulation designed for attendees to work through an incident-response problem with peers rather than only watch a presentation.

Can I ask speakers about my team's situation?

Yes. Small-group office hours provide direct access to speakers and experienced platform leaders, with room for questions that need more context than a standard session Q&A.

What does registration include?

Your ticket includes all conference sessions, the live teardown, the hands-on simulation, small-group office hours, lunch, and the evening gathering.
