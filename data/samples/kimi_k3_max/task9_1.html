// about

A day built around the people who keep systems up

Stackglass Live is a one-day, ticketed, in-person conference for platform engineering and reliability teams, taking place in Chicago on October 16, 2027.

Every part of the program is led by practitioners — people who run internal platforms, own reliability targets, and build the infrastructure and developer experience their organizations depend on. Expect field reports, honest trade-offs, and techniques you can take back to your team the following week.

No expo-hall shuffle, no parallel sales tracks. One room, one day, and a program designed to be worth every hour away from your dashboards.

Who it’s for

  • Platform & internal developer platform engineers
  • SREs & reliability engineers
  • Infrastructure & cloud engineers
  • Developer experience practitioners
  • Engineering leaders building platform teams

Four ways to spend the day

Every format is designed for depth over breadth — and every ticket includes all of it.

Practitioner Talks

Field reports from teams running internal platforms, reliability programs, infrastructure, and developer experience in production — what worked, what broke, and what they’d do differently.

Talks

Live Architecture Teardown

A real production system, dissected on stage. Trace how the architecture evolved as usage grew and the engineering organization scaled around it — the decisions, the trade-offs, the scars.

Teardown

Incident-Response Simulation

Get paged at Stackglass. Work a realistic incident in a hands-on, team-based simulation: triage, mitigate, communicate, and run the post-incident review — under realistic pressure, with coaching throughout.

Hands-on

Small-Group Office Hours

Direct access, small groups. Bring your hardest questions about platform strategy, reliability practice, and team design to speakers and experienced platform leaders.
