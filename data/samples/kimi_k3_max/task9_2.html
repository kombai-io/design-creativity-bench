The program

Practitioner talks. Zero pitches.

Five deep technical areas on one single-track stage. Every speaker builds and operates AI systems for a living — expect architectures, failure stories, and real numbers, not slideware.

01

Model Evaluation

Offline and online evals, golden datasets, LLM-as-judge pitfalls, and quality gates that catch regressions before your users do.

02

Retrieval Systems

Chunking, embeddings, hybrid search, and reranking — plus how to measure retrieval quality against real query traffic.

03

Inference

Latency budgets, throughput, caching, and batching — the cost curves that decide what actually ships.

04

Agents

Tool use, planning, and orchestration in production, and the guardrails that keep autonomous workflows boring — in a good way.

05

Production Reliability

Monitoring nondeterministic systems, incident response, safe rollbacks, and SLOs that make sense for AI features.

The postmortem, performed live

Live Failure Analysis

One production AI application behaved beautifully in testing — and very differently in production. In this session, the team that owns the system reopens the incident on stage, dashboards and traces in hand. Follow the investigation from the first alert to the root cause, and see exactly where the gap between testing and production was hiding.

  • Real dashboards, traces, and eval results — not a retelling
  • Where staging evals gave false confidence, and why
  • The fix, and the checks that guard the system today

Bring your laptop

Hands-On Evaluation Lab

A 90-minute guided lab where you build a working evaluation harness from scratch. Using sample model outputs and curated datasets, you'll define failure modes, label a golden set, score outputs against a rubric, and leave with code you can adapt to your own system the following week.

  • Sample model outputs and datasets provided
  • Hosted notebooks — no setup, no GPUs required
  • Mentors circulate for help and rubric review
