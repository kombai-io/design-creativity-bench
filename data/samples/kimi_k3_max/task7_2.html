How SceneLilt works

How it works

From one master cut
to every market.

SceneLilt turns a single finished video into launch-ready versions for each language you ship in — transcription, translation, dubbing, on-screen text, review, and export in one continuous workflow. Here’s the path your campaign takes.

  1. 01

    Upload & target

    Upload the master. Pick your markets.

    Drop your finished video into SceneLilt and choose the languages and regions you’re launching in. The platform instantly spins up a dedicated version for each market — all linked back to the original master.

    • Any resolution, codec, or aspect ratio
    • 32 languages with regional variants
    • Every version stays synced to the master

  2. 02

    Transcribe & detect

    Everything that’s said — and shown — gets captured

    SceneLilt transcribes the original dialogue with speaker labels and word-level timecodes, then scans every frame for on-screen text, titles, and captions that will need adapting.

    • Speaker-labeled, word-level transcript
    • On-screen text detected in every scene
    • Nothing to prep or type up by hand

  3. 03

    Translate, dub & adapt

    Each market gets its own cut, automatically

    Dialogue is translated for meaning and tone — not word-for-word — then re-voiced with natural dubbed audio. On-screen text is re-typeset in the target language, matched to your design.

    • Context-aware translation that keeps your voice
    • Studio-quality AI dubbing, lip-timed to the edit
    • On-screen text re-set in your brand fonts

  4. 04

    Compare & refine

    Compare with the original. Tune until it feels native.

    Play any localized version side-by-side with the master. Adjust wording, retime a line to the lips on screen, fix a mispronounced name, or shift the tone — every edit is non-destructive.

    • Side-by-side playback with the master
    • Language, timing, pronunciation, and tone controls
    • The original is always one click away

    Review & approve

    Local reviewers comment and sign off

    Invite in-market reviewers to leave timestamped comments on their version. Resolve threads, track every change, and collect approvals market by market — no feedback lost in email threads.

    • Timestamped comments on any frame
    • Independent approval status per market
    • Full revision history for every version

    Export & publish

    Export every version, ready to publish

    • Broadcast, social, and OOH formats
