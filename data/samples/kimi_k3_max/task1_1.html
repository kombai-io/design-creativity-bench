Common questions, quick answers

Can I switch plans or platforms later?

Absolutely. Upgrade from Analytics to Optimization, or add a second platform, at any time from your dashboard. Changes are prorated automatically — you only ever pay for what you use.

How does annual billing work?

You pay once per year and get 20% off every price (e.g., Optimization on Shopify drops from $2,000 to $1,600/mo). Your annual plan is still covered by the 30-day money-back guarantee.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled in one click with no fees and no notice period. Annual plans are fully refundable within the first 30 days.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), plus ACH or wire transfer on annual plans. All payments are processed over 256-bit encrypted connections.
