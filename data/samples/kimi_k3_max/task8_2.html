    How it works
    
      
    Patch real webhook traffic into a safe place to break it.
    
      
    No mocks to maintain, no tunneling hacks, no “it worked on my machine.” Point real providers at a Patchbay endpoint and see exactly what they send.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    01
    
        
    Create a temporary endpoint
    
        
    Spin up a unique URL like in.patchbay.cloud/pb_x4k2 in seconds. It starts accepting HTTP webhook events immediately — no deploy, no config.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    02
    
        
    Capture real events
    
        
    Register the URL with your payment, source-control, or e-commerce provider. Every request is captured with full headers, raw body, signature, and timing.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    03
    
        
    Replay, edit, and break it
    
        
    Forward events to your dev environment, modify payloads before replaying, and simulate 500s, timeouts, and slow responses — until your handler is boring.
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

    



    
  
    
    
    
      
    The platform
    
      
    Everything between “we got an event” and “it works in prod.”
    
      
    Purpose-built for the messy middle of webhook integration work — the part that usually lives in someone’s terminal history.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    
        
    Instant temporary endpoints
    
        
    Disposable URLs that receive events the moment they exist. Keep them for an hour of debugging or a sprint of integration work, then let them expire.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
        
    Inspect every event
    
        
    See raw payloads, headers, signatures, and delivery timing for each request. Diff two deliveries side by side to spot exactly what changed.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
        
    Replay on demand
    
        
    Re-send any captured event to localhost, staging, or CI with one click. Signatures are re-computed so your handler can’t tell the difference.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
        
    Modify payloads before replay
    
        
    Change amounts, IDs, states, and edge-case fields directly in the inspector. Test the weird invoice.failed you can’t trigger on demand.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
        
    Test failure scenarios
    
        
    Respond with 500s, drop connections, or add artificial latency. Verify your provider’s retry behavior and your own idempotency before production does.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
        
    Shared event history
    
        
    Every capture and replay lives in a shared timeline. Engineering and support debug from the same session — paste a link, not a screenshot.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
        
    Generated handler code
    
        
    Patchbay turns captured events into ready-to-adapt handler snippets — routing, parsing, and signature verification included. See it below.

	"encoding/hex"
	"encoding/json"

	"net/http"
	"os"
)

var signingSecret = []byte(os.Getenv("WEBHOOK_SIGNING_SECRET"))

func verifySignature(payload []byte, header string) bool {
	mac := hmac.New(sha256.New, signingSecret)
	mac.Write(payload)
	expected := "sha256=" + hex.EncodeToString(mac.Sum(nil))
	return hmac.Equal([]byte(header), []byte(expected))
}

    FAQ
    
      
    Questions teams ask before joining.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        When does the private beta open?
        
    We’re admitting the first cohorts now, in small waves. Joining the waitlist reserves your place in line — invites go out in join order, and teams with active webhook integration work get priority.
    
      
    
      
    
        What does the beta support at launch?
        
    The initial beta is focused on HTTP-based webhooks: temporary capture endpoints, inspection, replay with payload editing, failure simulation, shared event histories, and handler snippet generation. Non-HTTP sources are on the roadmap, not in the first cohort.
    
      
    
      
    
        Which signing methods are handled?
        
    Common schemes are supported out of the box — HMAC-SHA256, timestamped HMAC variants, legacy HMAC-SHA1, and static secret headers. Patchbay verifies signatures when events arrive and re-signs correctly when you replay them.
    
      
    
      
    
        Is it safe to send real webhook traffic to Patchbay?
        
    Endpoints are temporary and unguessable, all traffic is encrypted in transit, and event data is scoped to your team’s workspace. You control retention — purge a session’s history at any time. We recommend capturing test-mode or sandbox events during the beta.
    
      
    
      
    
        How does replaying to localhost work?
        
    You can replay through the dashboard to any reachable URL, or run the generated curl command from your own machine to target localhost directly — no agent or tunnel required.
    
      
    
      
    
        What does it cost during the beta?
        
    Nothing. The private beta is free for accepted teams, including shared workspaces for engineering and support. We’ll publish pricing well before general availability, and beta teams will get preferential terms.
