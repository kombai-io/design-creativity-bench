Core Curricular Tracks

The Five Hard Problems of Production AI

We bypassed general machine learning overviews. Every talk and session drills directly into battle-tested architectures and operational tradeoffs.

Track 01

Model Evaluation

Moving past generic synthetic benchmarks to domain-calibrated evaluation suites. Automated LLM-as-judge correlation, human-in-the-loop validation, and continuous drift monitoring.

Track 02

Retrieval Systems

Hybrid dense/sparse indexes, graph augmented retrieval (GraphRAG), real-time chunk invalidation, multi-tenant rerank latency, and cold-start context routing at 10M+ documents.

Track 03

Inference Optimization

KV-cache compression, speculative decoding, continuous batching architectures, vLLM / TensorRT deployment topologies, and GPU cluster cost economics under bursty loads.

Track 04

Autonomous Agents

Deterministic tool-use orchestration, runaway loop circuit breakers, state recovery across async RPC boundaries, multi-turn plan verification, and rollback protocols.

Track 05

Production Reliability

Graceful degradation waterfalls, semantic fallback models, schema-enforcement validation, token leak blast radiuses, observability traces, and zero-downtime prompt versioning.

Centerpiece Session • 11:15 AM CST

The Live Failure-Analysis: When Staging Lies

INCIDENT TICKET: #INC-8942-LLM

Almost every team has experienced it: an AI system that cleared a 99.4% benchmark in offline testing, then degraded into circular tool calls, semantic hallucinations, and 18-second latencies within hours of customer traffic.

In this unvarnished, live deep dive, the engineering leads will project real production traces, vector query logs, and telemetry graphs. Attendees will analyze step-by-step why the synthetic eval passed, where the distribution gap occurred, and the structural guardrails deployed to prevent recurrence.

Key Takeaway of this Session:

A concrete, reproducible checklist for stress-testing multi-step tool calls against adversarial edge-case inputs before deploying code.
