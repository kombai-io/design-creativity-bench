Signature 90-Minute Centerpiece

The Live Architecture Teardown

How does an engineering stack survive 100x traffic growth without collapsing under technical debt?

In this unvarnished, code-level deep dive, platform leads pull back the curtain on the multi-year evolution of an actual production platform. You'll examine the exact inflection points where naive architectures failed, what triggered emergency rewrites, and how the team transitioned safely without downtime.

  • Phase 1 to Phase 3 autopsy: From monolithic Postgres bottlenecks to distributed cell architectures.
  • The Breaking Point: Postmortem of a 4-hour cascaded failure under 800k concurrent connections.
  • Real git diffs & configs: Exact Terraform, Envoy proxy filters, and database sharding blueprints revealed.
  • Live Q&A: Grill the architects who made the calls (and paid the on-call tax).
