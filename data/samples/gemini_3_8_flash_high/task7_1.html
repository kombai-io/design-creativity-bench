Under the Hood

How Branchglass orchestrates production fidelity in your cloud

Every step happens in your AWS, GCP, or Azure account with transparent execution and enterprise security.

01 • WEBHOOK CAPTURE
Event Trigger
Developer runs git push. Branchglass evaluates branch rules, labels, and authorization before instantiating resources.
02 • COPY-ON-WRITE DB
Sanitized Storage Clone
Instant snapshot or copy-on-write clone of staging data. Built-in masking scrubs passwords, emails, and financial tokens on spin-up.
03 • DYNAMIC DNS & TLS
Public or Private URLs
Automatic wildcard SSL certificate and secure DNS routing. Add SSO, password gates, or restrict strictly to your corporate VPN.
04 • CLEAN TEARDOWN
Zero Ghost Spend
When the PR is closed or merged, our FinOps garbage collection destroys the database, cluster pods, and ingress rules immediately.
