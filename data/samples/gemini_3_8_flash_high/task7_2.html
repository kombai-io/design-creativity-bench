ANSWERS FOR CREATIVE TEAMS

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything your creative producers, localization managers, and legal leads need to know.

How does SceneLilt handle actor voice rights and consent?
SceneLilt requires verified talent consent protocols and adheres to SAG-AFTRA and international digital voice likeness guidelines. You can also select from our library of 500+ fully cleared, studio-grade enterprise synthetic voices if you prefer not to clone master talent.
Can we lock our proprietary brand terms and trademarks?
Yes. You can upload custom brand dictionaries, glossaries, and "do-not-translate" rules. SceneLilt enforces these constraints across all translation, subtitle, and neural speech engines with phonetic pronunciation overrides.
How does generative text adaptation handle complex motion graphics?
Our multimodal vision model tracks the position, perspective transform, lighting condition, and typeface weight of the master text. It inpaints the background clean and re-renders the localized string with identical motion paths and blending modes.
Can our local in-market teams review without creating accounts?
Yes. SceneLilt supports password-protected, time-limited magic links. In-market cultural leads can open the review link on desktop or mobile, scrub the synchronized video, leave timestamped suggestions, and click one-tap approval.
What export formats and audio stems are provided?
You can export ProRes 422/4444, MP4 (H.264/H.265), clean video without text, burned-in subtitles, and individual stems: localized dialogue stem, clean M&E (Music & Effects) track, and sidecar SRT, VTT, or XML subtitle assets.
Does SceneLilt support auto-reframing for vertical social ads (9:16)?
Yes. Our intelligent subject tracking automatically reframes 16:9 master compositions into 9:16 and 1:1 formats, keeping speaking subjects and key visual graphics centered in the safe zone for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
