Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch between AWS, Azure, or Both later?
Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade from single-cloud (AWS or Azure) to Multi-Cloud (Both) at any time directly in your account settings with a single click. Your settings, pipelines, and audit trails remain 100% intact, and bundle discounts are automatically applied prorated.
How does the 14-day free trial work?
You receive unrestricted production access to all features of your selected tier for 14 days without any charge ($0 due today). If you decide CloudOrbit isn't the right fit, cancel before day 14 with a single click in your dashboard and your card will never be charged.
What if we start on Monitoring and want Deployment later?
Upgrading takes 10 seconds. All the health baselines and drift detection data gathered during the Monitoring phase immediately feed into the Deployment engine's automated rollback intelligence.
How is the 20% annual discount applied?
When opting for annual billing, your effective monthly rate is discounted by a full 20% (giving you more than 2 full months free). You are billed once per year and your rate is locked in against any price changes for the entire 12-month duration.
Are there any cluster or deploy overage fees?
Zero overage fees. Both plans feature predictable flat pricing: unlimited clusters, unlimited deployments, unlimited pipeline concurrency, and unlimited team member seats.
Do you require root cloud credentials?
Never. We connect exclusively via temporary STS AssumeRole on AWS and scoped Azure Service Principals following the strict principle of least privilege. We are SOC-2 Type II and ISO 27001 audited.
