What becomes possible

When your sources, warehouse and analytics tools share one pipeline, the day-to-day changes.

01

Seconds instead of overnights

Change-data-capture streams row-level updates out of PostgreSQL and Kafka continuously. Your Snowflake tables reflect production as it happens — not as of last night’s batch.

02

One pipeline, governed end to end

Every route from source to BigQuery or Databricks runs through a single control plane: schemas tracked, retries automatic, failures alerted — and none of it living in a cron job nobody owns.

03

Analytics that trust themselves

dbt builds land the moment syncs complete, so Looker dashboards are never quietly stale. Analysts stop asking “when was this refreshed?” and start asking better questions.
