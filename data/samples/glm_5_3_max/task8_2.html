Capabilities

Everything you need between “provider sends it” and “production handles it”

Webhook failures are silent. Patchbay Cloud makes them loud, inspectable, and repeatable.

Temporary endpoints

Spin up disposable URLs in seconds. Point any provider at them, capture every event, then tear them down — no servers, no DNS, no fuss.

Replay & modify

Re-fire any captured event against your handler as many times as you need. Tweak payloads mid-flight to test edge cases your provider never sends.

Failure scenarios

Simulate timeouts, 500s, malformed bodies, invalid signatures, and duplicate deliveries — so your retry logic is tested before it matters.

Generated snippets

Turn any captured event into ready-to-use handling code for your stack — parsing, signature verification, and idempotency included.

Shared histories

Event logs and debugging sessions are shareable across engineering and support. One link tells the whole story — no screenshots required.

Signature-aware

The beta verifies common webhook signing schemes out of the box, so you can confirm your validation logic matches the provider’s spec.

Workflow

From zero to a verified handler in four steps

Create an endpoint

Generate a temporary URL per provider or per feature branch. Name it, tag it, and drop it into the provider’s dashboard.

Capture real events

Trigger webhooks from the provider — test mode, sandbox, or staging — and watch every header, payload, and signature arrive in real time.

Replay & break things

Re-fire events against your local or staging handler. Mutate payloads, inject failures, replay duplicates — on demand, not by accident.

Ship the handler

Export a generated code snippet for your framework, share the session with your team, and promote your integration to production.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is the private beta for?
Software teams actively building or maintaining webhook integrations — payments, communications, CI, SaaS platform events. If your handlers receive events from another system, you’re the target audience.
Does Patchbay Cloud ever touch my production traffic?
No. Patchbay endpoints are temporary and isolated for testing and debugging. You point providers at them during development, and promote your own verified handler to production when you’re done.
Which signing methods are supported at launch?
The beta supports common HTTP webhook signing methods — notably HMAC-based schemes with timestamped signatures used by major providers. Coverage will expand based on beta feedback.
Can I test failure scenarios like retries and duplicates?
Yes. You can replay any captured event, modify payloads before replay, and inject failures such as timeouts, server errors, invalid signatures, and duplicate deliveries — so your retry and idempotency logic gets exercised deliberately.
