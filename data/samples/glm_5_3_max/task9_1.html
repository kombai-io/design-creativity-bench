What is Stackglass Live?

A one-day, ticketed, in-person conference for platform engineering and reliability teams — built around practitioners sharing what actually worked, and what didn't.

Built for practitioners

Every talk comes from someone doing the work: internal platforms, reliability engineering, infrastructure, and developer experience — real systems, real trade-offs, real numbers.

Small on purpose

We cap attendance so office hours stay genuinely small-group and you can actually talk to the people whose work you came to hear about.

Interactive, not passive

Beyond talks, you'll take part in a hands-on incident-response simulation and watch a live teardown of a production architecture as it grew.

One day, well spent

A single focused day in Chicago — sessions, lunch, and an evening gathering all included with your registration. Fly in, learn, connect, head home.

Common questions

Is this event in person or online?

Stackglass Live is an in-person, one-day conference held in Chicago on October 16, 2027. There is no remote or hybrid option — the hands-on simulation and small-group office hours only work in person.

What exactly does my ticket include?

Registration covers all sessions — practitioner talks, the live architecture teardown, the incident-response simulation, and office hours — plus lunch on site and the evening gathering.

Do I need experience with incident response to join the simulation?

No. The simulation is hands-on but facilitated, and designed to be valuable whether you're on-call every week or just want to see how incident coordination works in practice.

Can I speak to speakers directly?

Yes — that's what office hours are for. Small-group office hours give you direct access to speakers and experienced platform leaders throughout the afternoon.

Why is attendance limited?

To keep office hours genuinely small-group and the event's conversations substantive. When tickets are gone, they're gone.
