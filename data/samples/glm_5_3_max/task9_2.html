Why attend

A conference for the people on the pager.

Modelcraft Summit is built around one question: what does it actually take to run AI systems in production? Every talk comes from an engineer with first-hand scars — not a keynote circuit.

Practitioner talks, no vendor decks

Speakers cover model evaluation, retrieval systems, inference, agents, and production reliability — grounded in systems they run today.

Learn by doing, not just listening

A hands-on evaluation lab and a live failure-analysis session put real model outputs and real incidents in front of you — not slideware.

Small rooms, direct access

Office hours are capped at small groups so you can ask speakers and experienced AI engineering leaders the question you actually came with.

Live analysis
The Failure Session

It passed every test. Then it hit production.

The centerpiece of the afternoon: a real AI application that behaved differently in testing than it did in production, dissected live on stage. You'll watch the divergence get traced from user reports back through logs, prompts, retrieved context, and evaluation results — and see exactly which assumption broke first.

  • Real timeline, real logs, real data slices
  • Why the test suite missed it — and what would have caught it
  • Audience participation in forming the fix
  • Take-home checklist for your own systems

The Evaluation Lab

Build an evaluation suite you'd actually trust.

A guided, hands-on lab using sample model outputs and prepared datasets. You'll work in pairs to design rubrics, run automated checks, argue about scoring disagreement, and end with a small suite that would have caught a known failure mode — because it's the same one from the failure session.

  • Sample model outputs and datasets provided
  • Pair-based — you leave with someone to compare notes with
  • Facilitators circulating to unblock you
  • No prior evaluation tooling experience needed

Questions

Frequently asked

What exactly does my registration include?+

Every pass includes admission to all sessions — the track talks on model evaluation, retrieval systems, inference, agents, and production reliability, the live failure-analysis session, the hands-on evaluation lab, and small-group office hours — plus lunch on-site and the evening gathering. Nothing on the schedule is sold separately.

Is the event available to attend remotely?+

No — Modelcraft Summit is an in-person, one-day event in Chicago. The hands-on lab, small-group office hours, and hallway conversations are central to the day, and they don't translate to a stream.

Do I need evaluation experience for the hands-on lab?+

No. The lab is designed to be approachable for engineers new to model evaluation and still substantive for people who run evaluation pipelines daily. Sample model outputs and datasets are provided, and facilitators circulate throughout the block.

How do office hours work?+

Sign-up sheets open the morning of the event at registration. Speakers and invited AI engineering leaders each host a small group, capped so that everyone gets airtime. Slots fill on a first-come basis, and office hours are included with every ticket.

Can I buy several tickets for my team?+

Yes — and if you're bringing three or more people from one organization, the Team Rate applies. Each attendee completes their own registration form so we can assign lab seats and office-hour groups.

What's the cancellation policy?+

Registrations are fully refundable until October 2, 2027 — two weeks before the event. After that, tickets may be transferred to a colleague at any time up to the morning of the event.
