What becomes possible

Connected systems, compounding outcomes.

01

Replication in minutes, not quarters

Stream change data capture from PostgreSQL into your warehouse as rows change — with schema drift detected and handled automatically, so downstream tables never silently break.

02

Transform while in motion

Trigger dbt models the moment a batch lands, so data arrives modeled, tested, and destination-ready — instead of sitting raw until someone remembers to run it.

03

One pipeline, every consumer

Fan the same trusted data out to Snowflake, BigQuery, and Databricks, and surface it in Looker dashboards your teams already live in — no more forks of the truth.
