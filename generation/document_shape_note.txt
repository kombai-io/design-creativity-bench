The `html` argument of submit_html must be the entire file, character for character: it must begin with `<!DOCTYPE html>` followed by the opening `<html lang="en">` tag, then `<head>` and `<body>`, and it must end with `</body>` and `</html>`. Do not omit the opening `<!DOCTYPE html>` and `<html>` lines, and do not start the document at `<head>`.
